Original description

Your • Tasks You will be responsible for the personal management and support of learners as a health specialist (selection, introduction, learning support, evolution discussions, etc.). You plan and coordinate the practical training of a health specialist as well as the assignments of the learners and ensure the quality of training in the field of nursing. You supervise and coach our vocational trainers, plan and carry out further training and ensure their vocational pedagogical competence. You will actively participate with the nursing training team in the development, evaluation and further development of cross-site training concepts in the field of nursing. You work in interdisciplinary and project-related teams and actively participate in specialist meetings. You will be involved in strategic planning for young talent and educational marketing and represent the management of vocational training and higher technical education in committees and external events. Your profile You have completed tertiary nursing training and at least two years of professional experience, ideally in the training area. A federal certificate as a trainer or an equivalent qualification is required. In-depth knowledge of Swiss educational systems as well as educational theories and didactics is available. You impress with your strong social, communication and leadership skills as well as your ability to resolve conflicts. You are characterized by an independent, responsible way of working with a high level of initiative and innovative, analytical thinking. You have experience in interdisciplinary collaboration, project work and active participation in committees and training concepts. Your advantages About us With around 1,400 employees, we ensure easily accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
