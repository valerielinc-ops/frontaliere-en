Main responsibilities

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you will take care of patients in inpatients in inpatients in the inpatients' own inpatients in the inpatients' inpatients' inpatients in the inpatients' inpatients' infirmary.
  • After a thorough training, you will be able to take a look at the entire spectrum of psycho-diatric diseases.
  • As an assistant psychologist or assistant psychologist, you coordinate treatments, plan therapies and perform test psychology examinations.
  • You participate in reports, case meetings and further trainings and are in close contact with the other treaters. Requirements:
  • You have a completed psychology degree (with foreign degrees, recognition by the PsyKo is required).
  • Your eugenically recognized psychotherapy training is advanced.
  • You have an assistance permit Psychotherapy in the canton of St.Gallen (authorisation to work under supervision) or you fulfil the necessary conditions to obtain them before taking up your job.
  • You like to work independently, be open-minded and sensitive and appreciate a dedicated team.
  • You have very good German knowledge (at least level C1).
