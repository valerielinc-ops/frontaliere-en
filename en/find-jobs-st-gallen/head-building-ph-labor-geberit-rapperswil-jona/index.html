Description

MAIN TASKS In our building physics laboratory, the foundations for innovative and high-quality Geberit products are created.

To manage this competence center (acoustics, statics, fire protection), we are looking for a technically experienced and strong leader with a passion for building physics, innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration.

The role as Head Building Physics Laboratory includes the following activities in particular:

  • You manage the building physics laboratory from a technical, business and personnel perspective (3 employees)
  • You are responsible for the building physics know-how in the areas of acoustics, statics and fire protection and with your team you ensure professional measurements, tests and external fire tests including approvals
  • You actively promote innovations, introduce new technologies and trends and make an important contribution to further development with innovation projects of our products
  • As a competent contact person, you advise and support product development and sales on demanding building physics issues
  • You prepare technical results in a way that is appropriate for the target audience and act as an interface to internal and external stakeholders
  • You maintain contact with universities and institutes and supervise and accompany interns PROFILE For this demanding and versatile task, you have a completed university degree (University/ETH) in building physics, civil engineering or physics or comparable disciplines with.
