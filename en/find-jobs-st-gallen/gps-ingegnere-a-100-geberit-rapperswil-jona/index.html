Role overview

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES In your role, you are responsible for integrating the principles of Lean Management (GPS) into the area of ​​maintenance in order to maximize the efficiency, availability and reliability of systems and machines.

This includes in particular the following activities

  • Implementation of lean principles (5S, CIP, etc.) in maintenance to reduce waste and increase efficiency
  • Development and implementation of preventive maintenance strategies taking GPS standards into account
  • Evaluation of system data via SCADA to identify weak points and initiate the necessary improvement measures and maintenance steps
  • Representation of maintenance viewpoints in production projects
  • Creation, review and introduction of maintenance checklists and plans
  • Carrying out root cause analyzes System malfunctions and development of sustainable solutions PROFILE You have a technical professional qualification (e.g.

automation technician) and further training in the technical field.

You are also characterized by the following characteristics and skills:

  • In-depth knowledge of maintenance processes, plant technology and preventive maintenance methods
  • Strengths in data analysis, problem solving and structured procedures
  • In-depth specialist knowledge of manufacturing and production processes
  • Proficient use of MS Office programs as well as good SAP processing knowledge
  • Independent work and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Team spirit, reliability and drive to perform
  • High commitment and decision-making ability ADDITIONAL INFORMATION We are considering this position exclusively direct applications.
