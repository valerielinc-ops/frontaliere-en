Cross-Border Community

Cross-border workers community forum: ask questions on taxes, permits, insurance, pensions and transport and get answers from people with hands-on experience.

By Frontaliere Ticino Editorial Team

Questions and answers between cross-border workers

The community groups questions into nine categories: taxation, permits, insurance, pensions, transport, residence, work, family and general. Each question has a title, a body, a category and tags; the list can be filtered by category and sorted by newest or most voted.

Reading questions and answers is open to everyone. Asking or answering requires signing in with a Google account.

Votes and accepted answers

Questions and answers can be upvoted, one vote per user, and a second click withdraws it; an answer can be marked as accepted. Your first question and your first answer each unlock an achievement on the progress page.

Answers come from other cross-border workers, not from advisers: for a personal tax case check the figures with the net salary calculator or ask for consulting.
