Role overview

The Psychiatric Services Thurgau offers integrated psychiatric care of all ages and for all disorders in the canton.

You work at Station Forensik 1, where patients are mainly accommodated in accordance with Article 59 STGB, and in the case of a corresponding indication, also in accordance with Article 60 STGB.

The station offers a supportive and activating daily structure and strives for a constructive, nonviolent and appreciative interaction with each other.
