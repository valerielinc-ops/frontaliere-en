Original description

The Thurgau Psychiatric Services offer integrated psychiatric care for all ages and disorders in the canton. You work on the Forensics 2 ward, where patients are mainly accommodated according to Article 59 STGB, and if appropriate, also according to Article 60 STGB. The station offers a supportive and activating daily structure and strives for a constructive, non-violent and appreciative interaction with one another. To complement the nursing team, we are looking for a qualified nursing specialist in forensics 80-100% from July 2026 or by appointment. Are you looking for a working environment that is more than just a place for daily work? With us you will experience an inspiring working atmosphere. Our corporate culture is characterized by cohesion, respect and appreciation. You will provide professional care for our patients as a reference person in the care process from entry to exit in an interdisciplinary team. Participation in relevant decisions such as easing and exit regulations. Planning and implementation of milieu therapeutic activities. Coordination of processes and tasks with pre- and post-care institutions/people. Completed training as a nursing specialist HF/FH with a focus on psychiatry, professional experience is an advantage. Commitment to personal training or further education to maintain a current level of knowledge.
