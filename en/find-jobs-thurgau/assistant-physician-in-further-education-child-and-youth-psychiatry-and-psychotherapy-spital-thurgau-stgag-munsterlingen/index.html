Role overview

For our psychiatric clinic in Münsterlingen and the outpatient adult psychiatry with clarification and triage centers, outpatient clinics, day clinics and outreach services at the locations Münsterlingen, Kreuzlingen, Romanshorn, Weinfelden and in the Frauenfeld Cantonal Hospital, we are constantly looking for assistant doctors in psychiatry and psychotherapy 100% or part-time.

We offer modern treatment for patients with clinical pictures from the entire spectrum of psychiatry.

You will take on psychiatric and psychotherapeutic activities in inpatient, day-clinic and outpatient treatment options under senior medical supervision Completed studies in human medicine (CH, EU or EFTA country) Interest in the subject of psychiatry and psychotherapy in all its diversity Enjoy working with mentally ill people in a multi-professional team High commitment, creativity and good knowledge of German The Thurgau Psychiatric Services (PDT) consist of the psychiatric department Münsterlingen Clinic, the outpatient adult psychiatry and the child and adolescent psychiatric service.
