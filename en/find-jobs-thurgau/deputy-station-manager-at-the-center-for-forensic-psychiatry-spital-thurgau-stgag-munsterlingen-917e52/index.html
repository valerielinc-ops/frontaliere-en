Role overview

For our team, we are looking for a dedicated nurse who supports the station management professionally, organizationally and in terms of personnel and, together with the team, ensures the quality of forensic psychiatric care.

If you have leadership skills, empathy and a passion for patient-centered care in an intraprofessional environment, we look forward to your application.

We are looking for a deputy station manager at the Center for Forensic Psychiatry 90-100% by 1 October 2026 or by agreement You work at Station Forensik 3, where patients are mainly accommodated in accordance with Article 59 STGB, and if the indication is made in accordance with Article 60 STGB.

Are you looking for a working environment that is more than just a place of daily work?

With us you experience an inspiring working atmosphere.

Our corporate culture is characterized by cohesion, respect and appreciation.
