Federal vote Jun 14 2026: civil service & 10‑million initiative (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 15 June 2026

On June 14 2026, Swiss voters will decide on limiting access to civilian service and on the initiative “No to a Switzerland of 10 million!”. Find details, procedures and deadlines.

On June 14 2026 the Swiss population is called to decide on two main items. Parliament has proposed limiting access to civilian service in order to strengthen the army. This measure has been presented as a response to the need to increase the armed forces. The left has expressed concern, fearing that civilian service could be weakened. ### Limiting access to civilian service According to the parliamentary text, the proposal aims to reduce the number of people who can perform civilian service, allocating more resources to military service. No precise numbers are given on how many positions would be cut nor which criteria would be used for selection. The stated objective is to have a larger and ready army. At the same time, the Democratic Union of the Centre has launched the popular initiative "No to a Switzerland of 10 million!". The initiative calls for limiting immigration from abroad so that the resident population does not exceed ten million inhabitants. The voting information booklet, published on April 24 2026 by the Federal Council, contains the official explanations on both items. Procedure for voting from abroad To participate in the June 14, 2026 vote from Switzerland, citizens residing abroad must first be registered in the Swiss electoral register. Registration is done at the Swiss representation (embassy or consulate) responsible for their place of residence. ### How to register in the electoral roll 1. Gather the documents: valid Swiss passport, possible identity card, proof of residence abroad (utility bill, rental contract). 2. Fill out the registration request form, available on the website of the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs or at the representation. 3. Send the form together with copies of the required documents, by registered mail or in person. 4. Await confirmation of registration, which usually arrives within a few weeks. 5. Once registered, receive the voting material by mail or go to the polling station if you are temporarily in Switzerland. ### Important deadlines The source does not specify a final date for registration, but advises to proceed well in advance of the voting day, namely June 14, 2026, to ensure receipt of the election package in time. ## Useful tool To check your income and understand any tax implications related to a change in employment status (for example, if you switch from civil service to a regular job), you can use our salary calculator. Frequently Asked Questions
When will the federal votes of 2026 take place?
The federal votes are scheduled for June 14, 2026, when Swiss citizens will be called to decide on two main issues: the limitation of access to civilian service and the popular initiative "No to a Switzerland of 10 million!"
What exactly does the initiative "No to a Switzerland of 10 million!" ask,
The popular initiative calls for limiting immigration from abroad so that the resident population in Switzerland does not exceed ten million inhabitants. The objective is stated simply, but the source emphasizes that implementation would be much less easy and does not specify which measures would be necessary.
How can Swiss citizens residing abroad participate in the vote?
Swiss citizens living abroad must be registered in the electoral roll. Registration is done at the competent Swiss embassy or consulate, presenting passport, proof of residence, and filling out the appropriate form. Once registered, they receive the voting material by mail or can vote in person if they return to Switzerland.
