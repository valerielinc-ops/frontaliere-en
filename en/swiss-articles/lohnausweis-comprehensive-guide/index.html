Lohnausweis: complete guide for border workers in Switzerland (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 26 June 2026

Everything you need to know about the Swiss salary certificate: what it is, how it is obtained and why it is important for border workers

Contesto

In short - The Lohnausweis is the Swiss salary certificate - It is mandatory for Swiss employers - It contains detailed information on income and deductions - It is fundamental for tax reporting ## Key facts - What : Swiss salary certificate - When: Issued annually by employers - Where: Switzerland - Who: Swiss employers - Amount: Not specified The Lohnausweis is a fundamental document for frontier workers working in Switzerland. It represents the salary certificate that Swiss employers are obliged to provide to their employees every year. This document contains detailed information on income received and deductions made during the tax year. For frontier workers, the Lohnausweis is particularly important as it serves as a basis for tax reporting both in Switzerland and in the country of residence. ### Information contained in the Lohnausweis The Lohnausweis includes a number of crucial information relating to the employee's employment relationship and tax situation. These include: - Annual gross income - AVS/AHV contributions - LPP/BVG contributions - Other tax and social security deductions - Any bonuses or additional fees

Dettagli operativi

Practical analysis The Lohnausweis is not only a formal document, but an essential tool to understand your tax and social security situation. For frontier workers, it is particularly important since the information contained in it is used by both the Swiss tax authorities and those of the country of residence. ### Implications for frontier workers - Double taxation: The Lohnausweis helps to prove the taxes already paid in Switzerland, facilitating the application for the tax credit in the country of residence. - Pension: Information on AVS/AHV and LPP/BVG contributions are crucial for planning old-age pension. - Income tax return: The document is essential to correctly complete the income tax return both in Switzerland and abroad. ### What to do if it is missing or contains errors If the Lohnausweis does not arrive or contains errors, it is critical to contact your employer or HR department immediately to resolve the situation. In some cases, you may need to contact the relevant tax authorities for clarification or assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions
When does the Lohnausweis arrive?
The Lohnausweis is generally sent by employers by the end of February of the year following the year to which the information relates.
What to do if the Lohnausweis contains errors?
If the Lohnausweis contains errors, you should contact your employer or HR department immediately for correction.
Can I get a duplicate of the Lohnausweis?
Yes, you can request a duplicate of the Lohnausweis from your employer if the original document has been lost or damaged.
