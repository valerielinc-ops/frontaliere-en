Concern among old cross-border workers: salary data sent to Italy includes errors and inaccuracies

Contesto

In brief - Salary data of old cross-border workers in tax returns - Error in data transmitted from Switzerland to Italy - Unions will seek clarification - What: Automatic transmission of salary data of old cross-border workers - When: 2026 pre-filled tax returns - Where: Ticino and Italy - Who: Italian cross-border workers employed in Ticino - Amount: Not specified The concern is high among old Italian cross-border workers in Ticino. Their salary data has appeared in the 2026 pre-filled tax returns of the Italian Revenue Agency. This event is due to the automatic transmission of tax information between Switzerland and Italy, which has included the data of workers hired before July 17, 2023, despite them being subject to a transitional regime of exclusive taxation in Switzerland. The situation has raised fears among workers, who fear possible subjection to Italian Irpef tax. Giuseppe Augurusa, national secretary of Cgil cross-border workers, stated that unions are working to remove this error and delete the salary data of old cross-border workers from Italian documentation. ## Transmission error The transmission of salary data occurred through the Common Reporting Standard (CRS), an automatic mechanism for the exchange of tax information developed by the OECD and applied between Switzerland and Italy. Under the new bilateral agreement that came into force in 2023, cantonal tax authorities are required to annually transmit the salary data of cross-border workers to the Italian Revenue Agency. However, the information flow was activated indiscriminately for the entire category, without previously excluding old cross-border workers, who are not subject to Irpef tax reporting obligations in Italy.
