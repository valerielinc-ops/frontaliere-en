Documents Needed to Start Working in Switzerland: Complete Guide (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 25 June 2026

Discover the complete checklist of documents required to start working in Switzerland as a border worker.

Contesto

In brief - Essential documents to start working in Switzerland - Employment contract and identity document - Request for G permit when applicable - Bank details if required by the employer - AVS registration and health insurance ## Key facts - What : Documents needed to start working in Switzerland - When: Before the start of work - Where: Switzerland - Who: Frontier workers - Amount: Not specified Starting to work in Switzerland as a frontier worker requires a number of essential documents. These include employment contract, identity document, G permit application when applicable, bank details if requested by the employer, AVS registration and health insurance. These documents are essential to ensure a smooth and legal transition in the Swiss labour market.

Dettagli operativi

Practical Implications For border workers wishing to start working in Switzerland, it is crucial to have all the necessary documents in order. This not only facilitates the recruitment process, but also ensures that workers comply with Swiss laws. For example, the G permit is required for frontier workers who reside in Italy but work in Switzerland. This permit allows them to work legally in the country and benefit from the rights of Swiss workers. In addition, it is important to note that bank details may be required by the employer for the payment of the salary. This means that frontier workers must have a bank account in Switzerland or Italy, depending on the employer's preferences. AVS enrollment and health insurance are equally important, as they ensure workers are covered in the event of illness or injury. ### Step-by-Step Procedure 1.
