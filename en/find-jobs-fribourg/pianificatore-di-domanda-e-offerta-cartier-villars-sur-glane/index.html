Main responsibilities

  • YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES Develop and refine accurate demand forecasts in collaboration with the subsidiary and marketing teams.
  • Develop procurement plans to ensure product availability, taking into account subcontracting allocations and lead times.
  • Ensure efficient and agile distribution of products, preventing the risk of product shortages and limiting carbon emissions.
  • Monitor and optimize stock levels to ensure optimal customer service while minimizing costs.
  • Proactively and transparently communicate planning results, analyzes and recommendations to internal and external stakeholders.
  • Analyze supply chain performance and suggest areas for improvement.
  • Actively participate in the optimization of our planning processes and tools.
  • WHAT YOU BRING You hold a Master’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Engineering, or a similar field.
  • You have at least 2 years of experience in demand, supply or distribution planning, ideally acquired in a demanding environment (luxury, consumer goods, manufacturing).
  • You master planning principles (MRP, DRP, S ) You have practical experience with one or more planning systems (SAP, ANAPLAN, etc.), and data visualization tools (Looker, PowerBI, or similar) You speak French and English fluently.
