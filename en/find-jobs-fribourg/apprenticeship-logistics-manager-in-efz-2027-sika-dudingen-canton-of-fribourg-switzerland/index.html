Role overview

For the start of the apprenticeship in August 2027, we are looking for interested young people for the apprenticeship as a logistics specialist EFZ.

Every day, a wide variety of materials are delivered and processed, and semi-finished and finished products have to be stored and shipped.

The tasks of the logistics specialist include the receipt of goods, incoming inspection, storage and transfer, inventory, warehouse management and the packing and securing of the transported goods, with the aim of providing the right quantity of the right goods in the right place on time and cost-effectively.
