Original description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs, and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna has set itself the goal of always being "simply better." Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of the Migros company. Tasks - You weigh the goods (pork, sow, beef, cow, and veal) at each cutting stage. You manage the weighing while respecting label requirements and ensuring product traceability. - You prepare and process fresh meat orders according to internal needs. You separate labels and manage orders via the SAP system. - You ensure rigorous monitoring of products to guarantee their traceability at every stage of the process. - You provide support in various sectors for tasks related to cutting, preparation, and product management.
