Role overview

Become part of something bigger.

Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life.

On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you. These are your tasks

  • Become part of something bigger.
  • Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life.

Contacts

  • You are the contact person and consultant for patients, external and internal customers (switch, telephone, e-mail) as well as insurance companies, cost carriers and suppliers with regard to our product range in the area of continence and everyday help.
  • You handle order processing (also export) as well as purchasing, warehouse management and article management.
  • You work closely with other departments of the clinic (therapy, care and purchasing).
  • Furthermore, you carry out the data administration in the ERP/CRM and perform further administrative tasks.
  • This sets you apart.
  • Ideally, you have an education as a pharmacist or specialist in health and bring experience in the commercial sector.
  • In a basic commercial education, you have several years of professional experience in the medical/paramedical field.
  • You like to advise clients, have no fear of touch and the joy of dealing with people distinguishes you.
  • In addition to German, you have good language skills in French and/or Italian.
  • Orthotec promotes the #free movement of people with paraplegia and similar restrictions through tools and services.
