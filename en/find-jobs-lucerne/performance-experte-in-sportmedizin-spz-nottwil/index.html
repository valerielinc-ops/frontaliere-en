Role overview

Become part of something bigger.

Work with over 2,000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable people with paraplegia to lead a self-determined life.

An inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary collaboration at eye level await you on the modern campus in Nottwil.

  • Become part of something bigger.
  • Work with over 2,000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable people with paraplegia to lead a self-determined life.
  • Creating and implementing individualized "evidence-guided" training programs in collaboration with the NLZ experts
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in the NLZ expert team (sports science, nutrition, psychology and medicine)

Main responsibilities

  • Creating and implementing individualized "evidence-guided" training programs in collaboration with the NLZ experts
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration in the NLZ expert team (sports science, nutrition, psychology and medicine)
  • Supervising the Para athletes in athletic units
  • Forming the interface between Para athletes and their trainers as well as the NLZ and performance experts
  • Deployment as part of the top sports RS of the Swiss Army's top sports promotion and, if necessary, in the training camps
  • Support with performance diagnostic test procedures in the laboratory and field Requirements:
  • Experience as a trainer with top athletes combined with professional trainer training
  • Studies in sports or movement sciences or training in sports physiotherapy
  • Holistic way of thinking with regard to promoting top-class sports
  • High communication skills and great flexibility

Company and context

  • Excellent skills in organizational, planning and coordination tasks
  • Language skills in German and an additional national language We offer: Not only attractive employment conditions await you, but also numerous others Advantages.

  • The Swiss Paraplegic Center in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for paraplegic, back and respiratory medicine.
  • As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the center offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives.
  • The clinic belongs to the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with spinal cord injury.
