Role overview

As a computer scientist / computer scientist "application development", you understand and describe the requirements of our customers and develop solution variants from them.

You create, design and implement databases, program software and test the functions.

Training is the focus in the first year, where you will independently solve exciting tasks and deal with the latest technologies.

At the end of the apprenticeship, you will have a broad education at a leading Swiss IT company for business software.

As a budding computer scientist or aspiring computer scientist, you have a quick apprehension, a good logical thinking, pleasure in working on the computer.

You love tricky and creative tasks and bring good math skills.

In addition, you have good to very good school grades, the focus being mathematics (Sec A).

Above all, your passion for numbers and thus also for computer science is important.
