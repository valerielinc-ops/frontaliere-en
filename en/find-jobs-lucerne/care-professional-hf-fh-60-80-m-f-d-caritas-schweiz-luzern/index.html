Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Care professional HF/FH 60-80 % (m/f/d) In the offer "Care for members" clients in need of care receive the best possible person-centered care by their relatives.
