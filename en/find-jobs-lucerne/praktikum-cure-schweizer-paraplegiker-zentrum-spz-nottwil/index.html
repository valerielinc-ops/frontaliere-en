Become part of something bigger.

Work with over 2,000 specialists from more than 100 professions and help people with spinal cord injuries live an independent life.

At the modern campus in Nottwil, you will find an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary collaboration on equal terms. These are your tasks:

  • Work with over 2,000 specialists from more than 100 professions and help people with spinal cord injuries live an independent life.
  • Assisting with personal care, positioning, and mobilization of patients with spinal cord injuries
  • Work actively in the care team after introduction and take responsibility for the tasks assigned.
