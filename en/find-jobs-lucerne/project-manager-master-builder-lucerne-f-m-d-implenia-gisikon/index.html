Role overview

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany.

Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets.

The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider.

Application process

  • Your field of application extends over Central Switzerland, where you are responsible for challenging building construction projects from the start of construction to the handover.
  • At the Gisikon site, Implenia offers the entire portfolio of services in building construction: from AVOR to execution and completion of complex new construction and modernization projects.
  • In doing so, we consistently focus on digital processes: calculation, extent and supplementary offers run via BauBit pro, BIM models and plans are shared with all project participants via Trimble Connect, and delivery notes are handled paperlessly via Comstruct.
  • You will work in an experienced, interdisciplinary team that aims to efficiently implement challenging construction projects with modern tools.
