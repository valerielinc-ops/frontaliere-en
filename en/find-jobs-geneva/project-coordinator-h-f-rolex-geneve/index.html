Role overview

Introduction For our Procurement, Support and Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a Technical Coordinator (H/F) whose main mission will be to ensure the interface role between maintenance teams, internal and external partners as well as individual applicants.

It will analyse, guide and manage technical requests, while facilitating exchanges, following action plans and ensuring the updating of associated data.

It will also contribute to continuous improvement of equipment performance, budget control and anticipation of technical risks associated with installations.
