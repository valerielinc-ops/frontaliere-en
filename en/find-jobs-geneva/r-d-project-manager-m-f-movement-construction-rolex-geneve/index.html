Role overview

Introduction For our Research & Development division, we are looking for an R&D Engineer in watchmaking design (H/F) with varied experience in the design of high-precision watchmaking movements incorporating complex mechanical systems and advanced technologies.

Attached to the "Construction Movement" service, you will participate in the phases of searching for concepts, construction and development of our future calibres.

Involved in the innovation and creativity phases, you will be at the heart of the development of new technical solutions that will meet the challenges of performance, reliability and exclusivity of our new products.
