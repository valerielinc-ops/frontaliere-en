Role overview

Attached to the management at the Home Hospital Centre (CHAD), the night home maintenance team (EDT) provides support to the day teams to offer the offer of late bedtime in order to promote home retention and the level of client autonomy.

The carer provides services independently or in pairs, respecting the customer's lifestyle, beliefs, values and culture at home.

He is required to travel throughout the canton of Geneva between 21:00 in the evening and 01:00 in the morning.
