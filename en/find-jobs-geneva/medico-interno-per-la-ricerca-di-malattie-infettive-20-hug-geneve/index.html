Role overview

You will participate in the national multicentre project iGASWISS-2, dedicated to the study of invasive group A (iGAS) streptococcal infections in Switzerland.

This project, carried out in collaboration with several academic centres across the country, aims to deepen epidemiological, clinical and microbiological knowledge of iGAS in order to improve prevention and management strategies.

Under the supervision of the principal investigators, you contribute to the scientific and operational activities related to the conduct of the study.
