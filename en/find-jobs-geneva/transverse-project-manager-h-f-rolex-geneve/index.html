Role overview

Introduction For our Production division, we are looking for a Transverse Project Manager (H/F) whose main tasks will be to develop a portfolio of optimization projects and ensure that the associated objectives are achieved.

You will have to pilot both operational projects, such as optimisation of production management methods, and more strategic projects for the company, such as the evolution of internal or external processes with an impact on sustainability.

The diversity of these missions will allow you to flourish in a stimulating environment, while developing diverse skills and a wide network.
