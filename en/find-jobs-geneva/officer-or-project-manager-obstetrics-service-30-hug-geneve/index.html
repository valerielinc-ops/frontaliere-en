Role overview

Under the responsibility of the Lead, Assistant Medical Officer, the selected person is responsible for the management and operational direction of the maternal and neonatal health (NMS) collaborative projects.

These projects target vulnerable pregnant women, including migrant women from low-resource countries, as part of the partnership between the HUG, partner associations and Madagascar.

It ensures the coherence, monitoring and quality of the activities carried out, under the leadership of the head, in close collaboration with national and international partners, respecting the values and institutional procedures of the HUGs.
