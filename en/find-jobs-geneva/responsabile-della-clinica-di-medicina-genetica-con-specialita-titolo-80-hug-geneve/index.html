The Genetic Medicine Service is looking for a clinical head with a specialty title (FMH or equivalent) to ensure, under the responsibility of senior physicians, full management of the genetic patient(s) (adult or child) in terms of both clinical diagnosis and interpretation of genetic analysis results, and genetic counselling.
