Role overview

Introduction For our Purchasing, Support and Infrastructure division, we are looking for a Methods Project Manager – Investment Maintenance Responder whose main mission will be to represent maintenance in investment projects, ensuring the preparation of machines for their future maintainability, monitoring their condition and performance as well as the evolution of associated needs.

His role will also be to manage the activities of the AS&I partners integrated into the projects.

As such, it must guarantee good visibility on the progress of investments within its scope, in particular through the monitoring of indicators and regular communication.
