Role overview

Within the Secretariat of the Policlinic d,oncologie, you are a specialist medical or medical secretary and actively contribute, on rotation with the team, to the coordination of the outpatient care path for patients.

In close collaboration with oncologists, nursing teams and internal and external partners, you contribute to the smooth running of the care and the fluidity of the patient path.

You ensure the reception, information and orientation of the patient and their relatives, as well as the admissions and recording of administrative data according to institutional requirements.
