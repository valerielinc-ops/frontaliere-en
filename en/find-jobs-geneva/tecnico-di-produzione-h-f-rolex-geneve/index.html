Role overview

Introduction For our Production division, we are looking for an Operations Technician (H/F) whose main mission will be to coordinate and supervise the various activities of the production workshop - machining and finishing.

Combined with the entire production process, it is able to integrate a multidisciplinary team of specialists and collaborate with support services.

He is a collegiate participant in the management and facilitation of the flow performance on the quality, cost and timeliness axes.
