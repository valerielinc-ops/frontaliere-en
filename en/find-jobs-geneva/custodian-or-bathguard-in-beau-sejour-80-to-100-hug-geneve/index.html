Original description

As part of your function, you ensure the supervision of the Beau-Séjour basin and the users, hosting daily a patientele in rehabilitation. You are kindly communicating by creating a climate of trust. You ensure the proper functioning of the facilities, the control and technical intervention according to the defined protocols, the control of the equipment, the order and the cleanliness of the premises (cleaning of changing rooms and other areas). You report any anomalies and ensure the transmission of the necessary information to the hierarchy. The position requires rigour, a strong sense of responsibility, good physical condition and the ability to manage emergency situations.
