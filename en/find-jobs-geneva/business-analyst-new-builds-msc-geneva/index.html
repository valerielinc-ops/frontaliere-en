Your Purpose The New Builds Business Analyst plays a key role in ensuring the financial, commercial, and operational success of MSC Cruises' new ship development projects. Acting as the financial and analytical partner to the New Builds department, this role is responsible for defining, monitoring, and optimizing departmental budgets, overseeing owner-supplied materials (OS&E and FF&E where applicable), and ensuring sound financial governance throughout the project lifecycle. Beyond budget management, the Business Analyst delivers data-driven analyses and recommendations to support decision-making across front-of-house and back-of-house initiatives. By evaluating investments, costs, operational models, and project performance, the role helps ensure that innovation, guest experience, and operational efficiency are achieved within financial objectives. Working closely with Finance, Procurement, Technical, Hotel Operations, Food & Beverage, Guest Experience, Design, and external partners, the Business Analyst provides insights that support specification decisions, supplier strategies, budget optimization, and overall project profitability. The role also develops reporting, dashboards, and KPIs to monitor performance, identify risks and opportunities, and ensure alignment between financial objectives and the strategic goals of the New Builds department.
