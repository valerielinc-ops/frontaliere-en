Role overview

Under the responsibility of the production leader, you are part of a team of group leaders, sharing the management of the 70 medical devices technologists in the department as soon as they take up their positions and on a daily basis.

You arrange and ensure the reprocessing of medical devices for all the services of the HUG (Operational Blocks, Care Services or any other in-house client) and external clients based on your customer-oriented attitude.

You coordinate the management of medical devices according to good sterilization practices, priorities and emergencies for operating units and all clients.
