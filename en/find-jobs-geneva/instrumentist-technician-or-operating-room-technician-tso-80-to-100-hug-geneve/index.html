Role overview

As an instrumentist, technician or operating room technician (TSO) in the operating rooms, you ensure the patient's reception, installation and safety.

You assist the surgeon or surgeon and you are in charge of the technical activities and the specific care of the operating room in compliance with the rules of good practice, especially in the field of the aseptics and hygiene rules.
