Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities will include:
  • Drafting documents: Preparing and publishing job advertisements (internally and externally), establishing employment references and contracts in both English and French.
  • Data management: Maintaining accurate employee records and administrative data within our HR systems.
  • Recruitment: Organising in-person and online interviews with international candidates and managing the onboarding of new employees.
  • Administrative support: Assisting with various administrative tasks related to recruitment, scheduling internal meetings and other HR initiatives.
