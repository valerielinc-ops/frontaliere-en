Role overview

The mobile social emergency unit (UMUS), made up of nurses and social workers, intervenes in situations mainly linked to violence and precariousness in coordination with the Geneva socio-health network.

The team works in pairs and operates from 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. on weekdays and 24 hours a day on weekends and public holidays.
