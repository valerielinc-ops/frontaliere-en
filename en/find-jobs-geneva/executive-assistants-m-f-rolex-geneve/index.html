Role overview

Introduction We are looking for Senior Management Assistants (H/F) whose main mission will be to assume a central and leading role with management by providing operational support in the fulfilment of its responsibilities.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinating and monitoring all administrative activities
  • Anticipate, organize and monitor workflows of work, information and communication in full autonomy, including through email management, calendar, calls and correspondence
  • Acting as a high quality interface between management and internal and external interlocutors
  • Expectation and availability to identify and implement solutions tailored to different situations
  • Manage specific files and projects online
  • Draft meeting records, coordinate and follow up decisions taken
  • Prepare presentations and report by collecting and analyzing data and results Profile
  • Commercial maturity or equivalent training, federal certificate of management assistant, an asset
  • A proven experience of more than 10 years as a senior management assistant in a large structure
  • French mother tongue with excellent spelling and perfect syntax
  • Excellent written and oral knowledge of English
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Strong capacity for anticipation, leadership and very good priority management
  • Personality with interpersonal, service and hospitality
  • High level of discretion and confidentiality Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
