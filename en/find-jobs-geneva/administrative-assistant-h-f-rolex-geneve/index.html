Role overview

Introduction For our Coordination & Administration department, we are looking for an Assistant (H/F) whose main mission is to provide high-quality administrative support to contract management activities and procurement interfaces, ensuring documentary compliance, proper application of processes and reliability of associated models and tools.

The position is part of a high value-added service that aims to support business teams through reliable, structured, digitalized and quality-oriented services.

Responsibilities

  • Provide support for administrative management of contracts
  • Participate in the management, updating and reliability of documentary models
  • Provide administrative support to the activity manager
  • Support internal users in a procurement-oriented environment
  • Ensuring the quality, consistency and conformity of the documents processed
  • Contribute to compliance and improvement of administrative processes, procedures and standards
  • Monitor applications in dedicated tools and contribute to smooth administrative processing
  • Collaborate with several internal interlocutors in a logic of outreach service and internal customer orientation
  • Contribute to the operational functioning of the service by providing cross-cutting support for coordination, communication, document management and monitoring of activities, with a proactive, reliable and collaborative approach.

Profile

  • Training: SWC trade employee, professional maturity
  • Confirmed administrative experience, ideally in a structured, demanding or supportive environment
  • Very good ease in document processing, administrative management and case tracking
  • High interest in standard processes, procedures and environments
  • Excellent mastery of Microsoft 365 tools: Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, OneNote, SharePoint
  • Strong ability to work with collaborative and digital tools
  • Knowledge of IVALUA, DocuSign and Visio is a strong asset
  • Interest in generic AI tools, including Copilot, with the ability to formulate prompts in a relevant and effective way
  • Very good organisational and prioritisation capacity
  • Support in the simultaneous management of several requests and interlocutors
  • Appetence for cross-cutting coordination, planning and support tasks
  • Service and confidentiality, rigour and reliability
  • Team spirit, adaptability, autonomy within the defined framework, relationship ease
  • Master's in English (B2).

Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
