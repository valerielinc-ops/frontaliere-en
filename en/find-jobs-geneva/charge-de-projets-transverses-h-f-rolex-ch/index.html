Introduction For our Production division, we are looking for a Cross-functional Project Manager (M/F) whose main missions will be to manage a portfolio of optimization projects and guarantee the achievement of the associated objectives.

The diversity of these missions will allow you to flourish in a stimulating environment, while developing varied skills and an extensive network.
