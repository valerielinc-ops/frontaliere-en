Role overview

The clinical specialists attached to or attached to the clinical expertise and professional practices of the Directorate of Care constitute a team of professionals expert in various areas of care and contribute through their actions to improve the practices and quality of care provided to patients within the institution.

At the University Hospitals in Geneva, the wound and healing program is attached to the clinician and professional practice expertise cluster of the Department of Care.
