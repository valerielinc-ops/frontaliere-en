Original description

For our clinic for surgery, we are looking for an assistant doctor as of January 2027. The hospitals Schaffhausen operate an interdisciplinary emergency center at the Cantonal Hospital. Together with the emergency practice led by family doctors, we offer comprehensive and high-quality medical care. With about 15,000 patients treated in a 24-hour operation (emergency center) and additionally approx. 7,500 patients in the emergency practice (18 – 10 p.m.) are treated and cared for more than 20,000 patients per year. Tasks: • The medical care of the assigned station • Operations under specialist instructions • Assistance in various operations • Regular care of surgical patients in the emergency department Requirements • Very good knowledge of German in language and writing (minimum requirement level C1) • Professional and social competence in dealing with patients and relatives • Commitment and commitment in a clinically interdisciplinary oriented activity • Responsibility, initiative, teamwork, resilience and independence • Swiss Federal Medical Diploma or equivalent MEBEKO recognition Please attach the employment certificates and diplomas and mention in the motivation letter the period in which you are available. We offer: Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well. The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
