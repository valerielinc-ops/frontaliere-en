How to compare sectors

Represented occupations depend on the sources included in the panel. A large listing count can reflect crawler coverage, republication or seasonality. This snapshot does not measure annual growth, candidate shortages or entry-pay premiums. Comparing sectors requires consistent definitions and coverage over several periods.

What the data tells us

The total describes the observed panel. Proving a historic record, contraction or positive balance requires comparable snapshots and removal data. These measurements are unavailable here and cannot be inferred from the current listing total.

Average pay depends on sample composition: occupations, experience, working hours and employers. It does not establish polarisation or a widening decile gap. Compare equivalent roles and conditions before drawing conclusions about personal income.

Geographic tables cover listings classified in Canton Ticino. A workplace alone does not establish eligibility for a permit or tax treatment. Consult the relevant guides and authorities for individual requirements.

Trends to watch

Monitoring subsequent periods requires comparable data:

  1. Preserve the observation date, source list and inclusion rules. Adding a crawler may enlarge the panel without indicating economic growth.
  2. Distinguish reported pay from estimates and check variable components, payment frequency and employment percentage. A bonus in one offer does not measure how common that benefit is across the market.
  3. Separate new publications, republications and removed listings. A disappearing listing does not prove a hire, just as continued publication does not prove a candidate shortage.

Methodology

The panel is filtered to Canton Ticino and publication year 2026. Employers and locations are grouped by the names available in listings; naming variants may remain separate. Unnamed listings remain in total counts but not the corresponding rankings. Salaries require reported provenance, explicit CHF currency and annual period, and finite ordered bounds from CHF 20,000 to CHF 300,000. Estimated, ambiguous and monthly amounts are excluded. No currency conversion or assumed thirteenth payment is applied. Salary rankings require three observations per group. Counts do not establish that every listing represents a distinct hire. Without comparable historical snapshots, neither growth nor weekly new-listing counts are calculated.

This page is an updateable sample, not a census. When citing results, preserve your access date and state the year, canton, sources and sample limitations. A processing date, where available, does not prove individual review of every listing.
