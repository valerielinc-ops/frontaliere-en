Your cross-border worker profile

Personal area for cross-border workers: save your profile and permit expiry, estimate your net salary, follow applications and saved jobs, export your data.

By Frontaliere Ticino Editorial Team

What the profile holds

The personal area requires signing in with Google, or with LinkedIn when it is enabled. The profile keeps your job title, age, family situation, cross-border status (G or B permit), municipality of residence, children and family members, workplace, preferred border crossing, language, gross salary in CHF and permit expiry date.

With this data the page shows a quick net salary estimate, a profile completeness bar and a reminder of your permit expiry, together with the applications you sent, the jobs you saved and your email and notification preferences.

Your data, under your control

The profile is stored in your account with a local copy in the browser. At any time you can download a JSON file with your profile, achievements and preferences, or delete the account: deletion removes the saved data, the local copy and the sign-in user. Details are in the privacy policy and on the data deletion page.

Without signing in you can still use the net salary calculator and the Ticino job board.
