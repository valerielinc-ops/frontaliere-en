Night works on via Clemente Maraini in Lugano

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 30 April 2026

From Sunday, May 3rd to Friday, May 22nd, night works on via Clemente Maraini in Lugano for the extension and reinforcement of AIL substructures.

Contesto

In brief

  • Nighttime work on Via Clemente Maraini in Lugano
  • From May 3 to 22, from 8 PM to 6 AM
  • Extension and reinforcement of AIL substructures

Key facts

  • What: Extension and reinforcement of AIL substructures
  • When: From May 3 to 22, 2024, from 8 PM to 6 AM
  • Where: Via Clemente Maraini, between the former FFS level crossing and number 9
  • Who: Lugano municipal police
  • Amount: Not specified

From Sunday, May 3 to Friday, May 22, from 8 PM to 6 AM, in the section between the former FFS level crossing and number 9 on Via Clemente Maraini in Lugano, work will be carried out to extend and reinforce the AIL substructures. Vehicle traffic will be managed by security personnel, and users are invited to respect the road signs and the instructions of the personnel in charge of traffic management.

The nighttime work on Via Clemente Maraini could cause temporary inconveniences for cross-border workers who use this road to get to work. It is advisable to plan alternative routes or to expect longer travel times during the work period.

Before the work, Via Clemente Maraini did not present particular restrictions on nighttime traffic. With the start of the work, cross-border workers will have to adapt to the new traffic conditions, which could affect travel times and road safety.
