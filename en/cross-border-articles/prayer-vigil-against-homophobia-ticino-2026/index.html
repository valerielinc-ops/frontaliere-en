Prayer vigil against homophobia, biphobia and transphobia in Lugano (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 7 May 2026

On the International Day against Homophobia, a prayer vigil in Lugano to counter discrimination and violence.

Contesto

In brief

  • Prayer vigil against homophobia, biphobia, and transphobia in Lugano
  • Appointment on May 22 at the Evangelical Reformed Church
  • Initiative promoted by 'La Porta Aperta – Spazi di inclusione'

Key facts

  • What: Prayer vigil against homophobia, biphobia, and transphobia
  • When: May 22, 2026, at 8:30 PM
  • Where: Evangelical Reformed Church of Lugano, Viale Cattaneo 2
  • Who: Group 'La Porta Aperta – Spazi di inclusione'
  • Cost: Free event open to all

On the occasion of the International Day against Homophobia, Biphobia, and Transphobia, the group 'La Porta Aperta – Spazi di inclusione' has organized a prayer vigil open to the entire citizenship. The appointment is for Friday, May 22, at 8:30 PM, at the Evangelical Reformed Church of Lugano, in Viale Cattaneo 2. The vigil is born as a moment of reflection, listening, and intercession for all those who, in different parts of the world, suffer discrimination, marginalization, or violence due to their sexual orientation or gender identity.

This initiative is part of the numerous similar initiatives that will take place in different cities around the world. The initiative is promoted by the group 'La Porta Aperta' with Azione Cattolica Ticinese and is in collaboration with various Christian realities present in the territory, such as the Reformed Church of Ticino and the Christian Catholic Church of Switzerland. Ministers of the Roman Catholic Church will also be present, as a sign of a path that, despite different sensitivities, wishes to continue in dialogue and mutual listening.

Promoting Inclusion in the Workplace

For cross-border workers, participating in inclusion events can be a way to promote values of respect and equality in the workplace as well. Strengthening Community Ties

Participation in events like the prayer vigil can help cross-border workers strengthen ties with the local community. This can be particularly important for those living in a country different from their country of origin. Punti chiave

How to Participate in the Prayer Vigil

To participate in the prayer vigil against homophobia, biphobia, and transphobia in Lugano, simply show up at the Evangelical Reformed Church of Lugano, at Viale Cattaneo 2, on Friday, May 22nd at 8:30 PM. Participation is free and open to everyone, regardless of faith or sexual orientation. No prior registration is required.

What to Bring

To participate in the prayer vigil, there is no need to bring anything special. However, it is advisable to bring a coat or a light jacket, as the vigil will be held outdoors. Additionally, you can bring a candle or a torch to light the path during the vigil.

Useful Information

For further information about the prayer vigil, you can contact the group 'La Porta Aperta – Spazi di inclusione' or visit the website of the Evangelical Reformed Church of Lugano. Additionally, you can follow updates on the event through the social media channels of the organizing group.

Promoting Inclusion in the Workplace

For cross-border workers who wish to promote inclusion in the workplace, you can participate in inclusion and solidarity events like the prayer vigil. Additionally, you can organize similar events within your own workplace to promote values of respect and equality. Promoting an inclusive work environment can improve the quality of life and relationships at work.
