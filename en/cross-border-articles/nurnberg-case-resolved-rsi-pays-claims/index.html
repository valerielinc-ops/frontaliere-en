The dispute between RSI and journalist Paola Nurnberg, who was fired from her position in 2023 following a Twitter post, has been resolved. This was confirmed by the union UNIA, which supported Nurnberg and stated that 'RSI has paid all the claims made.'

The final decision was reached 'at a rapid pace until the desired goal was achieved: the dismissal.' 'The arguments put forward by the Unia union, which clearly demonstrated the total disproportion between the violation committed and the punishment, were to no avail,' the union continues, 'and the RSI management was unable (or unwilling?) to even offer Paola Nurnberg alternative employment within a company that employs 1,200 staff.'

In the UNIA statement, the issue of harassment against her is also mentioned, for which an internal procedure had been initiated: 'the thesis we have put forward is that a female employee who had previously found the courage to report a superior was targeted.'
