According to TILO's data, the most frequented stations in 2025 were Lugano (4.2 million passengers), Bellinzona (2.5 million), and Chiasso (2.1 million). The expansion of the railway offer has also led to an increase in the use of public transport by commuters. The Chiasso-Zurich line was one of the most frequented, with over 10,000 commuters per day. The CBT, inaugurated in 2020, has significantly contributed to the increase in passenger traffic, reducing travel times between Ticino and the rest of Switzerland. The tunnel has also led to an increase in transport capacity, allowing more trains to run simultaneously. TILO has also invested in the modernization of infrastructure and the digitization of services. In 2022, the company introduced a new electronic ticketing system, which recorded a 20% increase in online ticket sales in the first year of use. For the future, TILO plans to continue expanding its railway offer and improving services for passengers. Investments are planned for the modernization of stations and the introduction of new trains. The company is also working to improve integration with other public transport modes, such as buses and trams.

Operational details

The increase in rail traffic in Ticino has been constant in recent years, confirming the positive trend already observed earlier. In 2019, TILO transported 16.7 million passengers, while in 2025 the number rose to 25 million, representing a 50% increase in six years. The daily average of passengers transported by TILO is around 76,400 people, with peaks recorded during morning and evening rush hours. This increase is also due to the introduction of new stops and the improvement of services. For example, the opening of the new Lugano Paradiso stop in 2022 increased the frequency of trains and reduced travel times between nearby locations. Furthermore, the introduction of the cadenced timetable has made the service more predictable and reliable, encouraging more people to use public transport. The new Lugano Paradiso stop recorded an average of 1,200 passengers per day in the first half of 2023, significantly contributing to the overall increase in passengers. Another factor that contributed to the increase in passengers was the adjustment of tariffs and commercial offers. TILO introduced a new system of monthly and annual subscriptions in 2023, which is more convenient for commuters, with an average saving of 15% compared to previous tariffs. The data show that the municipalities most served by TILO, such as Lugano, Bellinzona, and Chiasso, have recorded the largest increases in the number of passengers. In particular, the Lugano-Bellinzona line saw a 60% increase in the number of daily passengers compared to 2019. To manage the increase in traffic, TILO has implemented a series of operational measures, including the introduction of more frequent security checks and the training of additional personnel for the management of trains and stations. - More frequent security checks - Additional personnel for the management of trains and stations - Improvement of station infrastructure. For the future, TILO plans to continue improving its service offer, with the introduction of new trains and the modernization of existing infrastructure. The goal is to reach 30 million passengers by 2030, maintaining its position as a leader in public transport in Ticino. The Swiss Confederation has allocated 120 million Swiss francs for the improvement of the railway infrastructure in Ticino for the period 2025-2030, of which 40 million are specifically dedicated to the strengthening of the Lugano-Bellinzona railway line.
