Gambarogno: citizens win battle over construction fees (cross-border guide)

Di Valerie Linc · Published on 4 May 2026

The Expropriation Court has ruled on levies for sewer and purification works in Contone. Here's what changes

Contesto

In brief

  • Court rules on construction contributions in Gambarogno
  • Withdrawals for canalization and water purification works
  • Reductive update of the Pgs

Key facts

  • What: Decision on construction contributions
  • When: May 4, 2026
  • Where: Gambarogno, Canton Ticino
  • Who: Expropriation Court
  • Amount: Not yet specified

The Expropriation Court has issued an important decision regarding construction contributions in Gambarogno. The dispute concerned withdrawals for canalization and water purification works in Contone. The appealing citizens have achieved a victory, with the court ruling in their favor. The decision implies that the General Spatial Plan (Pgs) will need to be updated in a reductive manner.

Implications for cross-border workers

For cross-border workers residing in Gambarogno or neighboring areas, this decision could have a significant impact. Construction contributions are an important item in the family budget, and a reduction could alleviate the tax burden. Additionally, the update of the Pgs could influence urban development plans, with possible repercussions on the local real estate market.

Procedure and next steps

The Municipal Council of Gambarogno will now have to update the Pgs in accordance with the court's decision. Specific details on the reduced contributions have not yet been communicated, but they are expected to be announced in the coming weeks. Interested citizens should monitor the official communications of the municipality to stay updated.
