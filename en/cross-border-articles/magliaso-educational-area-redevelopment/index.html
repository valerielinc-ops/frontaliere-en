TL;DR

  • New elementary school construction starts in Magliaso.

Key facts

  • Cosa: Construction of new elementary school in Magliaso.
  • Quando: Construction started on March 26, 2024.
  • Dove: Magliaso, Switzerland.
  • Chi: Bonetti e Bonetti Architects.
  • Importo: 14.186 million francs allocated.
  • Scadenza: School ready for 2028/2029 school year.
  • Progetto: PIVOT project by Bonetti e Bonetti Architects.
  • Struttura: Two above-ground levels and one underground level.

Dettagli operativi

The new elementary school in Magliaso will be an example of innovation and sustainability in school building. The structure will be designed to be efficient and sustainable, with minimal impact on the environment. The school will be equipped with advanced technologies for energy and water management, allowing resource savings and a reduction in greenhouse gas emissions. The school will also have outdoor spaces for outdoor activities and student recreation. The 'PIVOT' project by the Bonetti e Bonetti architetti studio foresees the construction of a modern and functional elementary school, with a total of five classrooms. The structure will consist of two above-ground levels and one underground level. On the ground floor are located the atrium, the cafeteria, the teachers' room, and the classrooms for creative and music activities. On the first floor are the five classrooms for lessons. The school will be equipped with advanced technologies for energy and water management, allowing resource savings and a reduction in greenhouse gas emissions. The school will also have outdoor spaces for outdoor activities and student recreation. The Municipal Council had allocated a credit of 14.186 million francs for the construction of the new elementary school. Vice Mayor Fè Fossati expressed her hope that the new elementary school in Magliaso will be a symbol of the community's future. The new elementary school in Magliaso will be an important investment for the local community, offering a modern and functional educational environment for students. The 'PIVOT' project by the Bonetti e Bonetti architetti studio was selected as the winner of the architecture competition launched by the Municipality in autumn 2019.
