Bridge days and leave planning

When a public holiday falls on a Tuesday or Thursday, taking a single day off (the Monday or the Friday) gives you a four-day break. These are the best bridge days in Ticino this year.

Frequently asked questions

Which Ticino holidays does Italy not have?

Public holidays in Ticino but not in Italy: Good Friday, Ascension Day, Whit Monday, Corpus Christi, Saints Peter and Paul (29 June) and the Swiss National Day (1 August). On these days the cross-border worker does not work in Switzerland.

Which Italian holidays do not apply in Ticino?

Liberation Day (25 April) and Republic Day (2 June) are holidays in Italy but not in Ticino: on these days the cross-border worker works as usual in Switzerland.

Is the cross-border worker paid on Swiss public holidays?

Yes. Recognised cantonal holidays are paid non-working days under the Swiss contract, exactly as for workers resident in Ticino.
