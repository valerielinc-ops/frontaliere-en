Main responsibilities

  • You are actively involved in the commissioning of a new production building in the food sector.
  • You install, test and commission the electrical building infrastructure - from lighting to energy distribution to security systems.
  • You are responsible for the commissioning, parameterization and functional testing of the building management system (BMS / BMS).
  • You support the connection and commissioning of production and operating equipment.
  • You work according to defined processes, standards and approvals and ensure careful documentation.
  • You will be involved in solving commissioning and start-up problems in a solution-oriented manner and will work closely with interdisciplinary project teams.

Key requirements

  • You have knowledge of building management technology (BMS / BMS), ideally including visualization and fault reporting systems.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): You have experience in connecting and commissioning production and operating equipment.
  • You have basic knowledge of photovoltaic systems, inverters and grid connections.
  • You understand hygiene and safety requirements in the food sector and use testing and measuring equipment safely. A permit according to NIV 13/15 is an advantage.
  • You work in a structured, careful and documentation-oriented manner – even under time pressure.
  • You are a team player, take personal responsibility and are willing to do on-call duty. German (very good knowledge) French (advantageous)
