Main responsibilities

  • You create short, medium and long-term sales forecasts and ensure reliable demand planning.
  • You take into account relevant influencing factors such as promotions, seasonal fluctuations, weather influences as well as product range changes and innovations.
  • You manage stocks of finished products proactively and in compliance with the FIFO/FEFO rotation rules.
  • You identify shortage and overstock risks early on and actively contribute to minimizing rejects and food waste.
  • You coordinate daily with production plants, suppliers, sales and marketing in order to react flexibly to changes in demand or production deviations.
  • You support and co-moderate the S process and bring the interests of sales and production together in effective planning.
  • You monitor relevant KPIs such as forecast accuracy and bias, service level, reach, depreciation and adherence to the production plan and derive measures from them.

Key requirements

  • 3 to 5 years of professional experience in demand or supply planning: ideally in the food industry, FMCG environment or retail.

  • B. in the food sector.
  • Very good knowledge of an ERP/supply chain system such as SAP PP/MM/APO or SAP IBP.
  • Excellent Excel skills, especially in dealing with pivot tables, Power Query and ideally macros.
  • A deep understanding of the requirements of fast-moving consumer goods, just-in-time processes and fresh logistics.
