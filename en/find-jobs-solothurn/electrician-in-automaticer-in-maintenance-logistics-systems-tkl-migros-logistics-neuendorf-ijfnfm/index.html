Main responsibilities

  • Independently carrying out maintenance and servicing work on the fully automatic order picking systems
  • Ensuring trouble-free production operations in collaboration with internal and external bodies
  • Supporting conversions, new installations and commissioning of new systems
  • Repairing our machines and surrounding trades (such as stacker cranes, gates, lifters and transfer carts)
  • Support in the continuous improvement process (CIP)

Key requirements

  • 1-3 years of experience: Maintenance
  • Apprenticeship as an electrician, electromechanic, automation engineer or equivalent training in the electrical sector with professional experience, preferably in maintenance
  • Basic knowledge of control technology and experience in dealing with sensors and actuators
  • Understanding of troubleshooting in an automated work environment
  • Interest in new technologies, high willingness to learn, working independently and in a team as usual
