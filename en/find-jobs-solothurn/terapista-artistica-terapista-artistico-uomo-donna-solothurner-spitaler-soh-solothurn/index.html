Psarelli's Head of Special Therapy Tel: +41 (0)32 627 16 08 The Psychiatric Services (PD) are responsible for the extended basic care of the psychiatric community in the canton with around 450 employees.

In addition to emergency and crisis centres, comprehensive clarification, therapy and counselling services are available through the clinics for Psychiatry, Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics and through the children and youth of psychiatry.

The treatment is tailored to the individual needs of patients and is performed outpatiently, day-to-day or hospitalized.

The Psychiatric Services also cover the Forensic Psychiatry in the Canton.

The Psychiatric Services are teaching and research hospital of the University of Basel.
