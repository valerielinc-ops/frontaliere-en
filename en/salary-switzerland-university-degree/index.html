Education level is one of the strongest drivers of pay in Switzerland. For university / applied sciences the Earnings Structure Survey reports a median gross wage of ≈ CHF 9'500 per month, against a national median of ≈ CHF 6'788. The figure is gross, standardised and covers the whole economy: actual pay also depends on occupation, canton and experience. Frequently asked questions

How much do you earn in Switzerland with university / applied sciences?

The median gross wage is about ≈ CHF 9'500 per month according to BFS data. It is a whole-economy reference; the real amount changes by occupation, sector and canton.

Is the value gross or net?

It is the median gross monthly wage. For the cross-border net (contributions + withholding tax) use the calculator.

Source: BFS/FSO — Swiss Earnings Structure Survey (LSE) 2022. Median gross monthly wage, standardised, total economy (private + public sector). Indicative values.
