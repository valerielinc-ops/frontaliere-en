Role overview

Tasks

  • As a cook in the USB, you can expect an exciting working environment of a large company in the community gastronomy
  • You will learn how to prepare and cook food properly - from shopping to storage in strict compliance with hygiene guidelines
  • You work in the rotation system at the Post Saucier (meat, sauces), Entremetier (sides, vegetables, soups), Cold kitchen and patisserie
  • The different needs of our customers as well as a healthy diet are the focus and are important to you
  • Participate in the daily meals of around 700 patients and around 2000 meals for employees Requirements
  • You have completed compulsory schooling (level A – grade 5 in the subjects Mathematics/German or level E)
  • You enjoy handling food and cooking
  • You remain resilient in hectic situations and can organize well
  • You have a good sense of taste and smell
  • You have robust health (work mainly standing)
  • High quality and hygiene awareness as well as a sense of order are important to you
  • Team and service orientation are a matter of course for you We offer:
  • free sports courses (including gym), discounts for shopping, sports and leisure, employee parties, learning events and, and, and!
  • Soft start thanks to introductory week with getting to know the USB and the other learners
  • Active support from the vocational trainers, your team and older learners – #TeamUSB is there for you
  • Location in the middle of the city – fast on the banks of the Rhine and in the city centre
  • a beautiful hospital garden for chilling and refueling
  • Cozy restaurants & cafes with discounted prices for you
