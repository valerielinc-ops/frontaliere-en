Main responsibilities

  • Instruct, encourage and advance employees - and be a role model for them
  • You pay particular attention to the freshness of fruits, vegetables and bread and declare war on food waste with clever order planning
  • Order goods on time and in exactly the right quantity
  • Plan the work cleverly within the framework of the weekly plan and thereby ensure efficient processes in your store
  • Do everything to ensure that the numbers are correct - in terms of sales, personnel costs and inventory

Key requirements

  • Three or more years of professional and management experience: in the retail trade as a specialist, department or district manager
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail (food) or a related professional category would be ideal

  • Skill in planning and organizing tasks and a flair for interpreting key figures
  • Physically fit, some colis weigh up to 10 kilos
