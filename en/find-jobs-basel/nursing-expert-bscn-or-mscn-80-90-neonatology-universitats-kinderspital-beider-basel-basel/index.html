Role overview

The neonatology of the UKBB with 16 beds, including six respiratory facilities, intermediate care and special care in the rooming-in, is located in the immediate vicinity of the Gebärsaal of the Frauenklinik of the University Hospital Basel.

We look after neonates and newborns from the 24th SSW.

The care team consists of 60 employees in a total of 40 FTE.
