Main responsibilities

  • You are responsible for conversion and new construction projects for our supermarket branches across all SIA phases - from the initial planning to the successful opening
  • You coordinate general and total contractors, architects, specialist planners as well as internal interfaces (sales point planning, facility management, branch operations) and lead them in a goal-oriented manner through the entire project process
  • You monitor deadlines, costs and quality on the construction site and ensure compliance with the relevant standards and regulations (SIA, Fire protection, occupational safety, etc.)
  • You negotiate work contracts, check addenda and are responsible for cost control according to BKP, including the approval of invoices and construction accounts
  • You hold building owner meetings, document decisions and prepare regular reports for department and management
  • You accompany the rectification of defects, warranty periods and the handover to branch operations and thus ensure a smooth start to the new locations
