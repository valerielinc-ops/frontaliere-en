The Claraspital participates in the SomPsyNet care model funded by the Department of Health Basel-Stadt and Health Promotion Switzerland together with the University Hospital Basel and other hospitals in Basel to promote the prevention of psychosocial stress consequences in somatic acute care hospitals.
