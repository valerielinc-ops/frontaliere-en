Spitex Birseck is a future-oriented, progressive non-profit organization.

On behalf of the communities of Münchenstein and Arlesheim, we provide professional and comprehensive care and support so that our clients can live independently at home.

We are looking for an experienced and motivated personality as an employee planning 70 - 100% with immediate effect or by appointment.
