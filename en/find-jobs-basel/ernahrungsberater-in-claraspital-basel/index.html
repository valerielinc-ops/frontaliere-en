Role overview

The Claraspital's nutritional advice supports patients of the Tumor and Abdominal Center during their inpatient stay and, if necessary, also accompanies them in an outpatient setting after they leave.

We also specialize in caring for patients before and after bariatric surgery.

  • We also specialize in caring for patients before and after bariatric surgery.
