Frequently asked questions

When does school start in Ticino in the 2027/28 school year?

Lessons of the 2027/28 school year start on Monday, 30 August 2027 in all Canton Ticino schools, according to the official DECS calendar.

When does school end in Ticino in 2028?

Lessons end on Wednesday, 14 June 2028; summer holidays start the following day.

When are the 2028 Carnival holidays in Ticino?

Carnival holidays run from Saturday, 26 February 2028 to Sunday, 5 March 2028 (9 days).
