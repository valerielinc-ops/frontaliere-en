Marco Ferrari — Editorial profile — cross-border taxation

Editorial profile of the frontaliereticino.ch newsroom

This editorial profile gathers guides on taxation between Italy and Switzerland, with a focus on cross-border workers in Canton Ticino. It covers Italian 730 and Redditi PF returns, cantonal and federal withholding tax, IRPEF transfers and the Italy–Switzerland cross-border agreement. The newsroom follows communications from the Italian Revenue Agency, Ticino tax administration and the Ministry of Economy, and publishes analysis on deadlines, double taxation, net-tax calculations, the 20 km border area, new cross-border workers and transitional rules.
