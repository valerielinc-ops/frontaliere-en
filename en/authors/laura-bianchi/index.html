Laura Bianchi — Swiss social insurance specialist

Laura Bianchi specialises in Swiss social insurance for Italian cross-border workers in Canton Ticino. She covers AHV first-pillar pensions, occupational pensions, accident insurance and LAMal health cover, including the right of option available to eligible workers. On Frontaliere Ticino she writes guides on retirement pensions, vested benefits, second-pillar benefits when employment ends, pillars 3a and 3b and compensation-office procedures. She follows the AHV 21 reform, minimum occupational-pension conversion rates, health insurance premiums and cover according to residence, and Federal Council social-insurance decisions. She helps readers understand annual pension certificates and compare pension funds using cross-border/resident calculators.
