Role overview

Do you feel perfectly comfortable in the virtual universe and would you like to do programming and development of applications and websites?

So training in application development is exactly what you need!

During this demanding four-year basic training, you will acquire a variety of knowledge to successfully enter the dynamic universe of computing.

Your training - You will develop new applications and participate in the extension of existing applications.

  • You will be responsible for the development of design projects, the development of prototypes, the execution of tests and you will accompany the launch of applications.
  • You'll be responsible for software maintenance and quality assurance.
  • You will analyze software problems and develop solutions proposals in close collaboration with internal clients.
  • During the internal basic training of a year, you will develop your practical skills autonomously in the framework of modular teaching units and small projects.
  • After this extensive basic training, you will use and deepen your achievements in various practical courses – every six to twelve months you will change your field of work.
  • What you bring We are looking for enthusiastic and motivated young people who want to move things.
