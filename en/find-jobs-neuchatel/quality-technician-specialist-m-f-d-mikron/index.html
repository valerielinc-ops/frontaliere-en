Your main activities As a Quality Specialist, you are a key element of the operational quality team.

Your role is to identify recurring non-qualifications, prioritize them, analyze them and find their root causes, in order to put in place corrective and preventive action plans.

This is in line with our quality management system.

In addition, you provide support to the QCO team for internal and external parts control.

As a second for the mechanical part, you ensure a backup role for Team Leader QCO.

You also play a role in supporting quality process analyses and make daily the logic of continuous improvement.

  • Conformity of parts: You control parts manufactured internally and by our suppliers.

You know how to choose and use the means of measurement adapted to each measure.
