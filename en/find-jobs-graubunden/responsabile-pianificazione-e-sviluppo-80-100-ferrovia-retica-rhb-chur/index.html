Cosa puoi fare

  • You will lead the Planning and Development department in the Production division according to the values and management principles of RhB and continue the transformation into a process-oriented organization.
  • Together with your team, you are responsible for the integrated production and supply planning of RhB <5 years.

  • You have good knowledge and professional experience in project management and strategy implementation.
  • You have leadership experience, characterized by a trusting and responsible basic attitude.
