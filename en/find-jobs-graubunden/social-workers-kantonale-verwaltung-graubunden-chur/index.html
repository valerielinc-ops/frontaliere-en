Role overview

The cantonal social services department performs the task of personal social and addiction assistance on behalf of the municipalities and supports people in the development of economic social assistance.

It leads eight regional social services in the canton.

The regional social service Chur/Plessur/Imboden is responsible for 10 municipalities with a catchment area of 67 000 persons.
