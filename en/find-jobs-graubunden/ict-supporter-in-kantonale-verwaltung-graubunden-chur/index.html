Role overview

The Office for Industry, Trade and Labour (KIGA) currently employs around 135 people in six regions in the canton of Graubünden.

In addition to the public employment agency (RAV), we organize labour market measures for job seekers and run the cantonal unemployment fund.

In addition, we perform various tasks in the field of labour market and occupational safety.
