Main responsibilities

  • (cosa il candidato farà) -
  • You accompany employees and managers in personnel-relevant topics along the entire employee lifecycle and ensure that our HR processes function professionally, reliably and service-oriented.
  • You combine operational implementation strength with a clear view of quality, structure and further development.
