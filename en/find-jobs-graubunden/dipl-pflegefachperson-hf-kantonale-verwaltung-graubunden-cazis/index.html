Role overview

The Office for Correctional Services (AJV) Graubünden is an office of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden.

The AJV is responsible for the execution of sentences and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention.

In Graubünden, the enforcement of punishments and measures is regulated as a uniform subject area and holistic task in which various professional groups work in an interdisciplinary manner with people who have committed crimes in order to prevent further delinquency.
