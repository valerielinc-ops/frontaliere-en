Ergo Ticino Sagl is an ergotherapy studio based in Corso Elvezia, in the centre of Lugano. We operate in the areas of neurology, hand therapy, pediatrics, cognitive rehabilitation, mental health and pain therapy, with a person-centered approach and oriented to concrete, shared and measurable goals.

We are a small and dynamic structure and we look for a professional who wants to work with clinical autonomy and direct responsibility in taking care of their patients.

The role

Self-employment of its patients: evaluation, definition of therapeutic objectives, treatment, documentation and relations with the doctor.

Some of the treatments are carried out at home in Luganese.

Management of contacts with senders, sick cases, accident insurance and AI for authorizations and reporting.

Study location: Corso Elvezia 22, 6900 Lugano.

Requirements

Diploma of ergotherapist recognized in Switzerland: SUP title or foreign title with recognition of the Swiss Red Cross (CRS) already obtained. Applications with ongoing recognition procedure are also welcome, indicating the stage reached.

Clinical autonomy and sufficient experience to manage your patients independently. The location is designed for a profile with professional experience and not for new graduates.

Personal GLN number, or requirements to get it. The RCC number used for billing is that of the study.

Driving license, own car and availability to perform home treatments.

Excellent knowledge of Italian, spoken and written.

Constitute preferential title

Experience in one or more of the following areas: pediatrics, neurology, surgery and hand therapy, mental health and pain therapy.

Interest and willingness to gradually assume part of the coordination activities of the study.

Knowledge of further languages.

What we offer

Percentage of use between 60% and 100%, to be agreed according to the availability of the person. The contractual basis for 100% use is 42 hours per week.

Possibility to progressively increase the percentage of use according to the volume of patients, with semiannual verification and relative written formalization.

Patients already from the first month: a part of the portfolio is already active and will be transferred progressively.

A lean structure, with quick decisions and direct contact with the management, without intermediate hierarchical levels.

Kilometer allowance for professional travel.

Trial period of three months, with initial support to facilitate the inclusion in the study and organizational processes.

For those who reside in Italy, support in practice for obtaining permission G.

How to apply

Send your application to jobs@ergoticino.ch, attaching:

- curriculum vitae with photos;

- short letter of presentation;

- copy of the diploma;

- copy of CRS recognition, if in possession of a foreign title;

- number GLN, if already active.

We also ask for:

- the desired percentage of use;

- the period of notice foreseen by the current working relationship, so that we can assess concretely the possible date of entry;

- for those who have a foreign title, the current state of the CRS recognition procedure.
