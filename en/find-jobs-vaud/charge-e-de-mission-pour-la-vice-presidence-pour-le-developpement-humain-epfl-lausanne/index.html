Role overview

Mission Join EPFL's Vice-President for Human Development (VH) and become key management support on its most strategic issues.

At the intersection of analysis, coordination and advice, you prepare and follow key files that inform decision-making and advance HPV priority projects.

It is a position for a person who likes to understand complex issues, identify the essentials and turn analysis into clear recommendations—with rigour, discretion and sense of outcome.
