Role overview

The North-South Pediatric Permanence is a medical centre that opened on January 8, 2025 to accommodate children aged 0-16 without an appointment for surgical and medical emergencies 7 days a week.

The medical team is made up of experienced pediatricians, allowing for high quality and fast handling.

Modern and equipped rooms offer the possibility of X-rays and laboratory, as well as simple wound or fracture treatments.
