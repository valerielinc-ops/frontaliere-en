Original description

Biocleaning agent M/F – Hourly permanent contract Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a leading intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,000 employees. The Rennaz Hospital Center has more than 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two geriatrics and rehabilitation clinics as well as two medical hotlines. All employees work hard every day to provide quality care to patients, embodying the values ​​of the institution: authenticity, exemplarity and innovation. The Cleanliness, Hygiene and Environment Unit (UPHE), attached to the Facility Management department, brings together hospital biocleaning, linen room and waste management activities. Join our biocleaning teams! We are looking for poolists available to work, depending on their availability, in the various departments of our hospital and our two geriatrics and rehabilitation centers. The main missions of this position are to ensure replacements for short or long-term absences from the different sectors of the cleanliness, hygiene and environment unit on our 3 sites (Rennaz, Monthey and Vevey). The different sectors and times in which you are likely to work: • Hotel cleaning of common premises and management of services dedicated to staff (management of central changing rooms, automatic clothing dispenser, ESR building cleaning and on-call rooms) • Biocleaning of clinical services (hospitalization of patients, emergencies, gynecology, critical care, dialysis, pharmacy, operating room, imaging, etc.) • Waste management Hours: Monday to Sunday // 6:00 a.m. - 8:00 p.m. We require • A school leaving diploma and significant experience in a similar position • Experience in the medical and/or hospital field desired • Knowledge of hygiene and hospital risk management desired • Proficiency in French, oral and writing • Versatility: interventions on several sites • Motivation and rigor: active participation in maintaining the hygiene and safety of our premises • An ability to work in a team
