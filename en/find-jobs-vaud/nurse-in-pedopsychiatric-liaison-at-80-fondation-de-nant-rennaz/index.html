Role overview

In order to complete its team of the Psychiatric Service of Welcome to Emergency and Liaison (SPAUL) within our Service of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of Children and Adolescents (SPEA), the Foundation of Nant is putting to the competition a position of Nurse in pedopsychiatric liaison at 80% THE MISSION The SPPEA takes care of young people aged 0-18 and their families in the Vaudois East.

It guarantees accessible, continuous and child-friendly psychiatric care.

The mission of SPAUL is to receive, evaluate, care for all patients in crisis requiring emergency psychiatric intervention and to guide them in the care system.
