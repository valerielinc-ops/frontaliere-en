Role overview

A historic site, the Les Pâquis site housed the first infirmary in Morges in the 19th century, then the first hospital in the city, before being converted into an EMS in the 1970s.

With its renovated premises, the site accommodates part of the administrative functions of the EHC, such as human resources, finances, information systems.

The “les Pâquis” site will regain its medical vocation with the Covid-19 pandemic in 2020, becoming one of the most important testing and then vaccination centers in the canton.
