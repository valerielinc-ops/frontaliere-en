Nurse at 80-100% CDI, 80% to 100% Integrated into the heart of a large recognized institution, the Hospital of the Institution of Lavigny is one of the reference Romanesque centers for patients with brain damage requiring neurological rehabilitation, or suffering from epilepsy difficult to treat.

Opened on 1 July 2024, the University Neurorehabilitation Service (SUN) is an innovative structure linking two institutions, the CHUV and the Hôpital de Lavigny.

With 75 beds and nearly 200 collaborators, the SUN provides highly specialized and interdisciplinary treatment in academic neurorehabilitation, for more than 500 in-patients and nearly 2000 outpatients per year.
