Role overview

Mission For the student community as well as for EPFL student associations, the Student Relations Delegate (DP-RE) ensures dialogue with the student community and represents the interests of students.

It oversees the Office of Associative Affairs, whose mission is to accompany and support associations, maintain communication with them, provide them with relevant information and ensure their proper functioning.

The position is intended to support the DP-RE in carrying out its strategic and academic missions in connection with the student community and associations recognized by EPFL, as well as in the development of a student learning and living environment conducive to academic success, well-being and student engagement.
