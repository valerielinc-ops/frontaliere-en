Role overview

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Intercantonal Hospital of the Broye (HIB) provides a mission of acute care and rehabilitation essential to the broyard population.

A dynamic, community-based and committed health institution, HIB places the quality of services, interdisciplinary collaboration and the well-being of its teams at the centre of its priorities.
