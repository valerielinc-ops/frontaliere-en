Role overview

Mission The Institute of Chemical Sciences and Engineering (ISIC, https://www.epfl.ch/schools/sb/research/isic/) of EPFL is looking for a laboratory in chemistry to work within the Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Platform (ISIC-NMRP) and the Chemical Stores Platform (ISIC-CHSP).

You will have the mission to support routine solid phase NMR analyses for EPFL research laboratories and other users of our platforms.

You will be involved in the preparation of samples, measurements, troubleshooting and maintenance of instruments, and support to users.
