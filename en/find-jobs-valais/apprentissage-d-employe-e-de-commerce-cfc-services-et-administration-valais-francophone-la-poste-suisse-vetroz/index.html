Role overview

Do you have an innate sense of organization, appreciate the variety and would you like to discover different fields?

So training of employee or business employee in the Services and Administration sector is exactly what you need!

During this three-year basic training, you will acquire the knowledge you need to successfully enter the world of work.

Your training venue will be located in Vétroz.

Your training - As a business employee, you will be responsible for managing many operating processes.

  • Depending on the location and area of activity, you will have to write e-mails, mails, minutes and contracts, prepare reports and statistics, perform analyses or optimize work processes.
  • As part of your various assignments, you will discover the diversity of the big company that is the Post Office.
  • You will learn about fields as exciting as they are varied, such as staff, marketing, finance, logistics and organization.
  • The Post Office encourages the possibility of combining basic commercial training with professional maturity.
  • What you bring We are looking for enthusiastic and motivated young people who want to move things.
