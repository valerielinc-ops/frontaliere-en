Application process

  • The unit focuses on master data management, application support and BPE (Business Process Expert) / BPO (Business Process Owner) tasks within the digital QC systems.
  • As a BPO (Business Process Owner) or Business Process Expert (BPE) You are responsible for the local laboratory systems and their harmonization between the Basel/Kaiseraugst locations as well as the global interfaces.
  • Your goal is to ensure compliance and thus the safety of our products as well as the health of patients.
  • The possibility In this versatile role, exciting tasks await you and the opportunity to actively shape the digital future of our quality control:
  • Process Responsibility & Documentation: Responsibility for the processes of local laboratory systems including documentation, change management (change management) and deviation management (deviation handling).
  • System harmonisation: harmonisation of the local laboratory systems for Basel/Kaiseraugst with the global interfaces (including ASPIRE, EMPOWER, NEXTLab & SingleLIMS).
  • Continuous Improvement: Promoting improvements in local laboratory systems and developing, implementing and implementing QC strategies and objectives with local and global partners.
