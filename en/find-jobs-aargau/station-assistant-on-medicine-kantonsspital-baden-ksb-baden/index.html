Role overview

More than an organization – become part of our team.

Organization is in your blood and you keep the overview even when it gets hectic? Perfect!

As a station assistant, you are the first point of contact on our private station. You hold the strings together, coordinate the day-to-day work of the station and, together with a well-established team, ensure that our patients feel in good hands. No day is like the other – that’s what makes your job so exciting.

Administrative and organizational support of the station day.

Implementation of medical-technical and nursing activities by delegation, such as blood sampling, electrocardiograms (ECG), vital sign checks and support in personal care.

Close cooperation with the interprofessional team.

Co-responsibility for a professional and smooth process on the station.

You work exclusively in early service – also on weekends and public holidays.

Completed training as a specialist in health EFZ or medical practice assistant EFZ.

Joy in dealing with people and working together in a team.
