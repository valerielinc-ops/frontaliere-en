Role overview

In this role, you will design and optimize modern workplace services for Axpo’s nuclear energy computing with a focus on safety, automation and user experience.

As an ICT Senior Workplace Engineer, you take over the quality management responsibility for the AI Workplace environment – from technology to operation – and ensure reliable, stable and traceably controlled service delivery.

For the new nuclear energy informatics at the Leibstadt KKL, Beznau KKB and ZWILAG nuclear power plants, you provide workplace services that meet the high nuclear requirements for safety, compliance, availability and quality, use synergies with Axpo Group IT and ensure a consistent quality level on all devices.
