Frequently asked questions

How does the weekly tax quiz for cross-border workers work?
Each week the quiz selects 5 multiple-choice questions from a pool covering Swiss and Italian taxation, AVS/LPP contributions, LAMal health insurance and G/B work permits. Every answer includes a detailed explanation of the underlying mechanism, so the quiz works both as an assessment and a learning tool.
What is the AVS/AHV contribution rate charged to a cross-border worker in Switzerland?
The AVS (1st pillar) rate charged to the employee is 5.3% of gross salary. The employer pays an identical amount, for a combined contribution of 10.6%.
How many pillars does the Swiss pension system have and what do they cover?
The Swiss system rests on 3 pillars: AVS/AI (1st pillar, mandatory state pension), LPP (2nd pillar, occupational pension through the employer) and voluntary individual savings (3rd pillar, 3a/3b).
Can cross-border workers choose between Swiss LAMal and the Italian national health service?
Yes: within 3 months of starting employment, cross-border workers exercise the right of option between Swiss LAMal health insurance and the Italian national health service (SSN). The choice is irrevocable for the entire duration of the employment relationship.
How often must the G permit for cross-border workers be renewed?
The G permit is valid for 5 years for EU/EFTA citizens and is renewed automatically as long as the employment relationship with the Swiss employer continues, with no need to file a new application.
