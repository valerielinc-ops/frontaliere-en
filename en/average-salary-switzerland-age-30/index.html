Pay in Switzerland rises with age and experience. For the 30–39 age band the Earnings Structure Survey reports a median gross wage of ≈ CHF 6'600 per month, against a national median of ≈ CHF 6'788 across all ages. "Median" means half of workers earn less and half earn more — a more realistic reference than the average, less skewed by very high salaries. The figure is gross and standardised; to estimate what you would actually keep as a cross-border worker, after social contributions and withholding tax, use the calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 30-year-old earn on average in Switzerland?

The median gross wage for this age band is about ≈ CHF 6'600 per month according to BFS data. It covers the whole economy; actual pay varies by occupation, canton, sector and education.

Is this gross or net?

It is the median gross monthly wage. Net pay depends on social contributions and, for cross-border workers, on withholding tax: use the calculator for a personalised net estimate.

Source: BFS/FSO — Swiss Earnings Structure Survey (LSE) 2022. Median gross monthly wage, standardised, total economy (private + public sector). Indicative values.
