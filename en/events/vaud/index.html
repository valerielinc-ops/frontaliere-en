FAQ

How often is the Vaud events agenda updated?

The agenda is refreshed automatically every day, collecting new happenings published by the canton's official sources.

Are these events useful for cross-border commuters?

Yes. Many events take place in municipalities near the border and in Vaud's main towns, easy to reach for those who live in Italy and work in Switzerland.

How we collect events

Events are gathered automatically from public Vaud agendas and refreshed every day. Each event is attributed to a municipality based on the venue or the region given by the source. Always verify date, time and venue on the organiser's original page before travelling: times and availability can change.
