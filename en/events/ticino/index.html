Yes. Many events take place in municipalities near the border and in Ticino's main towns, easy to reach for those who live in Italy and work in Switzerland.

Events are gathered automatically from public Ticino agendas and refreshed every day. Each event is attributed to a municipality based on the venue or the region given by the source.
