FAQ

Why don't these events have a municipality?

The source does not give a precise municipality for these events in Bern. They stay reachable here with date, time (when known) and a link to the official site.

Is the event information official?

We mirror data from public agendas. For final times, tickets and details always check the organiser's page linked on each event.

How we collect events

Events are gathered automatically from public Bern agendas and refreshed every day. Each event is attributed to a municipality based on the venue or the region given by the source. Always verify date, time and venue on the organiser's original page before travelling: times and availability can change.
