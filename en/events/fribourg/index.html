How often is the Fribourg events agenda updated?

Are these events useful for cross-border commuters?

Yes. Many events take place in municipalities near the border and in Fribourg's main towns, easy to reach for those who live in Italy and work in Switzerland.

How we collect events

Events are gathered automatically from public Fribourg agendas and refreshed every day. Each event is attributed to a municipality based on the venue or the region given by the source.
