Role overview

Master's degree in training in bioelectronics for the development of Organ-on-Chip miniaturized bioreactors The professional university school of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI), competes in a position as a Bachelor in training Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) with applications in bioelectronics and systems for the development of Organ-on-Chip mini-based bioreactors at the Institute of digital technologies Employment rate of 62.50%.

Entry into service 1 October 2026 or date to be agreed.

Responsibility and activity - Collaboration with research activities in the Medical Devices area (DM) of the MeDiTech Institute, with particular reference to the development of electronic systems applied to experimental devices and platforms in the biomedical field.

In particular, the candidate will contribute to

  • development and integration of electronic control and data acquisition
  • support for the design and implementation of monitoring and measurement systems
  • integration of electronic subsystems, sensors and actuators
  • development of firmware and communication interfaces for the management of devices
  • participation in functional tests and experimental laboratory activities
  • support for the collection, analysis and management of experimental data In particular, the position offers:
  • A fixed-time contract for the duration of the MSE master part-time.
  • Involvement in applied research and experimental development - An interdisciplinary and international working environment.
  • Sharing activities with qualified researchers.
  • Opportunity to form new skills, both practical and theoretical.

Applicants will be taken into account only applications submitted by 31 August 2026 through the application form, accompanied by:

  • CV
  • Letter of motivation
  • Diplomas
  • Ev.

certificates of study and work (if in possession) For further information, please contact Igor Stefanini (Responsible Area).

Incomplete applications will not be accepted, sent to different addresses or beyond the agreed deadline.
