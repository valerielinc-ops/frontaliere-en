Tissot Sales Associate - Swatch Group The Company Swatch Group is the world's first manufacturer of finished watches. With its 16 hourly brands, the Group is present in all price segments, and is also active in the production and sale of jewelry, movements and hourly components. Swatch Group combines, among other companies, the following hourly brands under its roof: Breguet, Harry Winston, Blancpain, Glashütte Original, Jaquet Droz, Omega, Longines, Rado, Union Glashütte, Tissot, Balmain, Certina, Mido, Hamilton, Swatch and Flik Flak. The Group has a strong multi-brand distribution and sales network with two sophisticated boutiques called Tourbillon and Hour Passion. Swatch Group is also an important player in nanomechanical, nanoelectronic and Bluetooth technologies essential to watchmaking and other industries.
