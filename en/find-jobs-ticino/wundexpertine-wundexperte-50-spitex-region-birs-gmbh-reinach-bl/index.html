Role overview

Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organization Spitex Region Birs GmbH.

Are you ready for an exciting challenge in an exceptional and diverse working environment?

With flat hierarchies and respectful cooperation at eye level, we place great value on professional skills and strong social skills.

To strengthen our WEX team, we are looking for you as a wound expert (50%) Mansioni

  • You work on a day-to-day basis from Monday to Friday
  • You are part of a small team of wound experts who, in a self-organized manner, look after our clients at all locations in the area of wounds
  • As a specialist for wounds, you are responsible for competent and resource-oriented basic and treatment care for our clients in the area of wounds
  • You are responsible for a certain number of For clients in the area of wounds, case responsibility including planning, control and monitoring of the care process
  • You maintain interdisciplinary collaboration with relatives, doctors and other services and are the contact person for wound questions for our employees.
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF
  • Further training as a wound expert
  • Several years of professional experience and broad specialist knowledge
  • An independent, responsible way of working
  • Open-mindedness, flexibility and Empathy
  • Willingness to travel by car
  • Cosa offriamo
  • No work on weekends and no night work
  • We take your personal needs into account when organizing working hours
  • Room for initiative and professional development
  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working atmosphere With us you will not only find a job, but a meaningful task with perspective!

Become part of our dynamic team and shape the future of healthcare with us.
