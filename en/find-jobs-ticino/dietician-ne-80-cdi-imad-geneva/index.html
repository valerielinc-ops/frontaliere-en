Original description

As a dietitian, attached to the dietetic service of the Nutrition and Dietary Centre, your activities will mainly include health promotion and prevention, clinical nutrition at home and collective restoration. You will work with the various teams of IMAD, including home maintenance teams, IEPA and Repit Temporary Reception Units (UATR), as well as the partners of the social health network and the municipalities. Your interventions are based on Evidence Based Practice (EBP) and terminology, and the Nutrition Care Process (TPSN). Your main activities will be: • Actively contribute to maintaining or restoring the health status of patients of all ages by providing nutritional care for preventive, curative, palliative, educational or rehabilitation purposes; • Intervening in patient clinical routes by actively collaborating with all key partners to promote continuity and quality of care; • Contribute to the home maintenance of the Geneva population, its quality of life and well-being by promoting its nutritional health through health promotion and prevention; • Exercising an expert role for the institution in the field of home health and nutrition care; • Provide practical training for HES students and training for other professionals in home nutrition and dietetics and/or health promotion/prevention; • Promote efficiency, quality and adequacy of health benefits. We are looking for a person with a bachelor's degree in nutrition and dietetics or equivalent (for foreign degrees, CRS recognition required), with 2 to 5 years of experience in clinical nutrition, including gerontology and/or community health. An interest in mental health and addictology would be an asset. An in-depth knowledge of the Geneva health and social network, combined with excellent capacities to work in both autonomy and interprofessionality, is essential. Your commitment, relationship qualities and solution-oriented approach will ideally complement your profile. Deadline for application: 17.06.2026 E
