Main responsibilities

  • As a health specialist, you will take on a varied and responsible role in the outpatient sector.
  • You accompany our clients in their familiar environment and make an important contribution to their quality of life and independence with your work. Your main tasks include:
  • Care and support for our clients in their home environment
  • Support with basic and treatment care according to care planning
  • Observation and forwarding of relevant changes to the nursing specialists in charge of the case
  • Electronic documentation of the services provided
  • Helping to design professional, appreciative and client-oriented care
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with relatives, doctors and internal and external specialists Requirements What you bring with you:
  • Completed training as a FaGe EFZ or FaBe EFZ Treatment course
  • Ideally, you already have experience at Spitex
