Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role The TS Sales Director sets the regional sales objectives for the TS product line, (including the corresponding targets for order intake, gross margin and sales), develops and implements winning strategies for projects, provides guidance to local sales teams, defines key customer accounts and actively develops customers relationships, leads the forecasting process on a monthly basis and acts as the Product Line interface to subregional sales teams for the region. This role will also be actively involved in Product Line campaigns, working with the global team to develop sales strategies, define target markets and technical capabilities required (locally or globally) to reach sales targets Main tasks • To evaluate all current and relevant information on market trends, customers and competitors and set the appropriate sales targets at a regional and sub-regional level in conjunction with the Regional Head. • To create sales and action plans for TS, identify key projects, define actions for regional and local teams, follow through until project closure • To develop, expand and maintain sales channels for TS through direct customer contact, via agents and/or within the Sulzer organization • To advise the Regional Head on market volume, sales budget and the ways of working the market, lead budgeting activities across the region to help develop and set targets and strategies for subsequent years. • To identify opportunities to sell the entire TS service/product portfolio according to the sales plan • To lead the regional sales team in providing TS product expertise when required at a sub-regional or country level to meet sales targets • To work with the TS Product Line on introducing new services/products to the region • To work with regional colleagues
