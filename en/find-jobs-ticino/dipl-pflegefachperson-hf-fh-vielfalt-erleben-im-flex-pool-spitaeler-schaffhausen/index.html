With our in-house crèche directly on the grounds of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you in achieving a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but is good for you.

The Schaffhausen hospitals include the cantonal hospital, the psychiatric services, the Posthof Neuhausen radiology and cardiology practices and the Schaffhausen Center neurology practice.

With over 1,700 employees, we ensure expanded basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region.

Every year we care for around 11,000 inpatients and 90,000 outpatients.

We are also an important training center in the canton.
