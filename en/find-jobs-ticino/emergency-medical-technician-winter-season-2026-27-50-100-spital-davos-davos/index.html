Original description

Our hospital-based emergency service is IVR-recognized and performs around 1,500 primary and secondary interventions with its emergency vehicles. In winter, the focus is on traumatology and there is close collaboration with the ski rescue services. As a regional/acute hospital with emergency service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for the basic medical care of the Davos population and guests. However, the well-being of our employees is equally important to us. Working with us means being in a small but extremely important operation for the region.
