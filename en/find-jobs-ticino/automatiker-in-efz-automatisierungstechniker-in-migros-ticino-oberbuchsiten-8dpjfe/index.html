Main responsibilities

  • You build automation and control systems and put them into operation.
  • You actively support the commissioning of the new production building in the food sector.
  • You integrate and network production facilities, conveyor technology and peripherals.
  • You parameterize and test interfaces between machine, line, GLT/BMS and IT.
  • You analyze faults, correct errors and support maintenance in complex problems.
  • You optimize control programs and ensure documentation, data backup and version management.

Key requirements

  • You have in-depth knowledge of PLC technology: automation and control systems.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as an automatic technician EFZ or a comparable qualification.
  • You can read electrical and automation schemes and use them safely.
  • You are familiar with common automation environments such as Siemens TIA Portal, Rockwell or Beckhoff as well as industrial communication standards such as Profinet, Profibus, Ethernet/IP or OPC UA.
  • You convince with independence, reliability, teamwork, readiness for pikett service and a solution-oriented way of working - even in demanding commissioning phases. German (proof of negotiation) French (advantageous)
