Company and context

  • Within SUPSI, the Department of Business Economics, Health and Social Care (DEASS) promotes interdisciplinary approaches to complex societal challenges through close collaboration with institutions, organizations, companies, and communities.
  • The Competence Centre for Management and Entrepreneurship (CMI) conducts applied research, executive education, and knowledge-transfer activities in the fields of management, entrepreneurship, organizational innovation, sustainability, governance, and organizational transformation.
  • The Centre aims to contribute to the development of responsible, innovative, and socially sustainable organizations and systems, generating impact both locally and internationally.
  • CMI combines rigorous scientific research with strong engagement with practice.
  • Its activities focus on organizational and institutional transformation, innovation processes, entrepreneurship, leadership, sustainability transitions, governance systems, and the management of complexity in public and private organizations.
  • The Centre is characterized by strong connections with regional stakeholders and institutions while maintaining an active presence in national and international academic and professional networks.
