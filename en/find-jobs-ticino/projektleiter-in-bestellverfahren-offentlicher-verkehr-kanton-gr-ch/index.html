Role overview

The Office for Energy and Transport is the cantonal competence centre for energy and public transport issues.

In the area of public transport, we are responsible for the strategic planning and the mutual coordination of all public transport in the Canton of Graubünden.

We coordinate the public transport of trains and buses and are responsible for the planning, project planning and implementation of projects for future-oriented public transport.
