Role overview

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES In your role you are responsible for the maintenance, repair and maintenance of the raw material supply as well as the production facilities and machines.

You carry out preventive maintenance measures to ensure system availability.

This includes in particular the following activities

  • Error analysis and troubleshooting on mechanical, hydraulic and pneumatic systems
  • Documentation of the work carried out and maintenance of the maintenance database
  • Participation in the optimization and improvement of production processes
  • Support in the installation and commissioning of new systems PROFILE You have a degree as a polymechanic EFZ or a comparable training and have several years of professional experience in the areas of maintenance.

You are also characterized by the following characteristics and skills:

  • Sound knowledge of mechanics, hydraulics and pneumatics, as well as specialist knowledge in manufacturing and production processes
  • Analytical skills and structured approach
  • Proficient use of MS Office programs and good SAP knowledge
  • Independence, a high degree of personal responsibility and commitment
  • Team spirit and reliability

– 8:30 p.m.); remaining working hours in daily working hours ADDITIONAL INFORMATION For this position we only consider direct applications.
