Role overview

The opportunity To strengthen our front-end sales organization, we are looking for a Head of Bid & Proposal to take on a central role in the Swiss sales team.

In this role, you will be responsible for bid management and the creation of high-quality and competitive offers for our portfolio of high-voltage products and transformers.

You will further develop the Bid & Proposal team and lead it both professionally and disciplinarily.

As a central interface, you coordinate all relevant internal stakeholders and make a significant contribution to the successful development of the Swiss market with timely, high-quality offers.

We are looking for an entrepreneurial leader with a proven track record of achieving goals and building high-performing teams in a dynamic growth environment.
