Head of Microtechnic Group - Swatch Group Head of Microtechnic Group The company Swatch Group is a leading player in world watchmaking. Its well-known watch brands cover all price ranges and are the ambassadors of Swiss Made watches on all markets. As the group's central research laboratory, Swatch Group R&D, Asulab division, is responsible for developing technically innovative products, subsystems and watch components as well as manufacturing technologies that are essential for their production. This is achieved through in-house projects in various technical areas and through active collaboration with universities and external research institutes. Job description We are looking for a Head of Group – Microtechnique who will join a multidisciplinary R&D company to develop components, systems and innovative products for Swatch Group companies. Main tasks: Responsibility and management of a small team of multidisciplinary and clock engineers Supervision and promotion of innovative projects in the field of watches and watch movements, mechanical and electronic Development of original concepts and technologies to improve precision and/or autonomy of watch movements Development of characterization systems and measurement Planning and monitoring of several projects, preparation of annual budgets Development and monitoring of academic and industrial partnership projects Application of methods related to quality standards Preparation of technical and administrative reports Technical reference in the field of watchmaking for the Directorate Intellectual development, creativity and innovation Profile EPF engineer (or equivalent) in microtechnics or physics, a PhD is a more Creative spirit, visionary and not afraid to get into the unknown Strong interest in innovation and advanced technologies Experience in the design and manufacture of electronic and mechanical watches Experience in multidisciplinary academic or industrial projects Technical curiosity and dedication Profession
