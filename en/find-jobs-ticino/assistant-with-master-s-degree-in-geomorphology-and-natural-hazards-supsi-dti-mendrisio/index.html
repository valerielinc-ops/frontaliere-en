Main responsibilities

  • and activities The candidate will have the opportunity to take part in the research, mandate and service activities of the Climate Change and Territory Competence Center (CCCT), a reference point at the southern Alpine level in the field of applied geomorphology, where he will have the opportunity to develop and perfect the following skills:
  • Cartography, monitoring and modeling of slope movements in the Alpine environment
  • Assessment of natural hazards and risks of geomorphological origin
  • Remote sensing monitoring, in particular through the autonomous acquisition of drone images and the processing and analysis of aerial and satellite images (especially satellite radar interferometry)
  • Requirements We are looking for a person with a solid academic background, motivated to work in an applied research and development context. Required:
  • Master's degree in geosciences or related fields
  • Knowledge in applied geomorphology/geology, with particular emphasis on the alpine environment
  • Experience in acquiring monitoring data in the field and from remote sensing
  • Knowledge and experience with Territorial Information Systems (GIS)
  • Experience with the main remote sensing data processing software (aerial and satellite)
