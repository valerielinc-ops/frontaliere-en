Tasks

  • follows the users for whom socio-educational intervention is necessary, individually or in small groups, in the framework of a therapeutic plan agreed with the team regarding the minor or his family
  • follows the users of the SMP for whom an educational or therapeutic placement in institutions for minors is necessary
  • collaborates with other bodies or institutions in taking care of psychosocial situations concerning the users reported to the SMP
  • carries out consultancy for bodies or institutions that deal with minors and for teachers and to other operators who work in contact with minors
  • participates in moments of sharing the clinical and training activity of the team and undertakes ongoing training
  • Requirements
  • SUP social operator diploma (option: educator) or equivalent qualification
  • if in possession of a foreign qualification, recognition by the SERI (State Secretariat for training, research and innovation)

  • aptitude and motivation to work with children and adolescents
  • previous experience in the juvenile field in the psychological and psychiatric field
  • knowledge of the territory, of the social services and of the socio-health network of the Canton of Ticino
