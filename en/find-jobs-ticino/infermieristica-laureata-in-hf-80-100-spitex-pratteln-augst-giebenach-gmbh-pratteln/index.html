Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organization Spitex Pratteln-Augst-Giebenach GmbH.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for you as Dipl.

Nurse HF (60-80%) What to expect

  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working atmosphere
  • A modern, rapidly growing and innovative organization With us you will not only find a job, but a meaningful task with a perspective!
