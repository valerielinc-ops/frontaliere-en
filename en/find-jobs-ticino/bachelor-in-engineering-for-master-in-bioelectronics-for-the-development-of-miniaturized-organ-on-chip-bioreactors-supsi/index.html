Role overview

Bachelor Assistant in Master of Science training in the field of bioelectronics for the development of miniaturized Organ-on-Chip bioreactors The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) is offering a position as Bachelor Assistant in Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) training with applications in the field of bioelectronics and system electronics for the development of miniaturized Organ-on-Chip bioreactors at the Institute of Digital Technologies for Personalized Health Care (MeDiTech) of the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) based in Lugano.

Entry into service 1 October 2026 or date to be agreed.

  • Responsibilities and activities - Collaboration in research activities in the Medical Devices (DM) area of ​​the MeDiTech Institute, with particular reference to the development of electronic systems applied to experimental devices and platforms in the biomedical field.

In particular, the candidate will contribute to

  • development and integration of control electronics and data acquisition
  • support for the design and implementation of monitoring and measurement systems
  • integration between electronic subsystems, sensors and actuators
  • development of firmware and communication interfaces for device management
  • participation in functional tests and experimental laboratory activities
  • support for the collection, analysis and management of experimental data
  • Requirements To best fill this position, the following main requirements are required:
  • Enrollment or intention to enroll in the MSE Master
  • Bachelor's training in electronic engineering or similar discipline
  • Good skills in analogue and digital electronics
  • Basic knowledge in the development of embedded systems and data acquisition
  • Familiarity with sensors, actuators and measurement systems
  • Interest in biomedical applications and interdisciplinary systems
  • Knowledge of the English language
  • Good communication skills and aptitude for team work The following are preferential qualifications:
  • experience with microcontrollers and embedded systems
  • programming knowledge (C/C++, Python or equivalent)
  • familiarity with programming tools electronic laboratory
  • interest in control and automation systems
  • We offer SUPSI offers a stimulating environment and unique opportunities for those who wish to grow professionally and actively contribute to innovation.

In particular, the position offers: - A fixed-term contract for the duration of the part-time MSE master's degree.

  • Involvement in applied research and experimental development activities - An interdisciplinary and international working environment.
  • Sharing activities with qualified researchers.
  • Opportunity to train new skills, both practical and theoretical.
