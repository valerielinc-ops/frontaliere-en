Role overview

For the opening of our new forensic-psychiatric station with an intermediate security level, we are looking for an experienced leader from the police, prison, forensics or a comparable security-relevant environment.

In this responsible key role, you will manage the security area professionally and operationally and shape a professional, safe and respectful environment together with your team.

You combine strong safety competence with understanding of dealing with mentally ill people.

Application process

  • We are on the move digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage.
  • As soon as your application has been received online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail.
  • We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen.
  • Then we will contact you.
  • During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges.
  • In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together.
  • You will also have the opportunity to get to know your future team.

Company and context

  • You work closely and trustfully with the clinic management, the nursing area, the medical profession as well as external partner organizations such as the police, emergency services and other authorities.
  • Together with the nursing director forensics, you will develop security-relevant concepts and guidelines within the framework of the PSG and ensure implementation in your area.
  • By carrying out risk analyses, safety assessments and appropriate prevention measures, you identify potential hazards at an early stage and initiate effective risk mitigation measures.
  • You plan, organize and lead regular training sessions as well as practical training sessions in order to sustainably strengthen the reliability and expertise of employees.
  • When setting up the new forensic-psychiatric station, you will actively contribute your expertise and significantly shape processes, security standards and organizational processes.
  • You have an associated vocational training as well as a recognized training in the security sector (preferably VSSU, security specialist with a federal certificate or comparable qualification) and you know the requirements of security-relevant organizations from practice.
  • Several years of experience in a security function with leadership responsibility enables you to lead teams professionally and set the right priorities even in demanding situations.
  • In emergency, situational and crisis management, you will bring in-depth knowledge and practical experience and also keep an overview under pressure.
  • Through your professional, calm and secure appearance, you also act calmly and solution-oriented in challenging situations.
  • The respectful and clear treatment of mentally ill people is a matter of course for you, focusing on professional de-escalation and appreciative communication.
