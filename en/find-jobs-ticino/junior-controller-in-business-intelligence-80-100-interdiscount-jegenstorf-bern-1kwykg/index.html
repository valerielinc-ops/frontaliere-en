Main responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the information and data-based control of central value creation areas along the procurement market, processes and sales market.
  • You prepare relevant data, analyzes and models in such a way that key areas of action become visible and informed decisions can be supported.
  • You create ad hoc analyzes, reports and KPI dashboards and continuously develop them to create transparency about sales, goods flow, promotions, procurement, processes and performance.
  • You analyze and validate data, recognize patterns, deviations and optimization potential and translate these into understandable decision-making principles for departments and management.
  • You support the prioritization and control of measures by using data and models to show where action is needed and what effect possible measures can have.
  • You work on interdisciplinary projects, clarify requirements with the departments, coordinate analytical tasks and present results in a target group-oriented and action-oriented manner. Requirements
  • You have a completed degree or comparable training in data science, statistics, mathematics, business informatics, computer science, business administration/economics or a related field.
  • You have strong analytical thinking skills and enjoy structuring, analyzing and comprehensibly preparing economic questions based on data.
  • You have a good foundation in data analysis, data validation and quantitative methods.

Application process

  • Initial experience with Python, SQL, BI tools or comparable analytical applications is an advantage.
  • You are interested in business processes, procurement and sales markets, and how data and model-based insights can contribute to better decisions.
  • You work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented manner, set priorities and move confidently between daily business, ad hoc inquiries and project tasks.
  • You are team-oriented, have strong communication skills and can present analytical results in a target group-appropriate, understandable and action-oriented manner.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English.
