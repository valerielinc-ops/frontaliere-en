Are you ready to shape the digital working world of tomorrow?

With you, we will shape the digital working world of tomorrow at Swiss Post – simple, effective and user-centered.

Company and context

  • You have already worked successfully in complex organizations and understand how to operate across different levels.
  • You not only think conceptually, but also actively ensure the implementation and visibility of the results.
  • You are used to taking responsibility for implementing initiatives and making progress visible.
  • You are familiar with agile working methods, work in a structured and data-based manner and quickly recognize connections and derive concrete actions from them.
  • You will use knowledge of qualitative and quantitative user research as well as usability to specifically optimize the digital employee experience.
