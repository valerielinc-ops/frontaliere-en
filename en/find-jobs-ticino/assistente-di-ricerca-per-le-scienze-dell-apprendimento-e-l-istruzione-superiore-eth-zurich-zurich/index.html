Role overview

Research Assistant for Research on Learning Sciences and Higher Education 10%-30%, Zurich, fixed-term print Drucken The Professorship for Learning Sciences and Higher Education at ETH Zurich strives to advance both the fundamental understanding of human cognition and learning and the application of this knowledge in real-world educational contexts, with a particular focus on higher education.

The group is looking for a Research Assistant who will support members of the lab in their research activities, including tasks such as data collection, data preparation, literature review, and analysis.

Currently, we are looking for someone to contribute to a specific project, as outlined in the task description.

However, we are also open to, and would welcome, collaboration beyond this project.

Job description

  • Support in data collection: You will help prepare and conduct data collection activities, such as organizing study materials, coordinating sessions with participants, and recording interactions
  • Qualitative data analysis of video recordings: You will analyse segments of spoken language and gestures for non-literal use of language describing concepts in mathematics Profile
  • You have solid mathematics knowledge at high school level or higher
  • You are a student at ETH or UZH (in any field)
  • You speak English fluently
  • You are motivated to learn about research methods in educational psychology
  • No prior experience with qualitative data analysis or data collection is expected Workplace Workplace We offer
