Role overview

The Analytics, Reporting, Data & Platforms area contributes to increasing the value of the Migros Group through data.

To do this, we build a modern data architecture on a cloud-based tech stack.

Our team is responsible for the Data Foundation in the data mesh setup: source-aligned data products as a reliable basis for all domains.

  • The Analytics, Reporting, Data & Platforms area contributes to increasing the value of the Migros Group through data.
  • To do this, we build a modern data architecture on a cloud-based tech stack.

Main responsibilities

  • end-to-end development and operation of source-aligned data products (including Item master and transaction data) including clearly defined SLOs, quality guarantees and high discoverability
  • Use of generative AI and agentic approaches to accelerate the development of data products and to automate operational processes
  • Definition and implementation of automatically verifiable data contracts as well as ensuring interoperability and reliability between source-aligned and consumer-aligned data products
  • Development, operation and further development of automated data pipelines in the Google Cloud stack (dbt, SQL, Airflow, BigQuery) as well Establishment of ELT processes using CI/CD
  • Development of data models based on Data Vault 2.0
  • Responsibility for viable architectural decisions across multiple data products as well as coordination with PO, stakeholders and the Requisiti team
  • Several years of experience: in data engineering and data modeling, including proven in a role with technical responsibility for data products or platforms
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in computer science, mathematics, physics, business informatics or a comparable training
  • Proven knowledge of data engineering with modern data warehouse and data product technologies such as SQL, Python, dbt, BigQuery and Terraform, ideally on Google Cloud
  • Practice in the development of data products and data contracts as well as a good feel for trade-offs between quality, reusability and time-to-market
