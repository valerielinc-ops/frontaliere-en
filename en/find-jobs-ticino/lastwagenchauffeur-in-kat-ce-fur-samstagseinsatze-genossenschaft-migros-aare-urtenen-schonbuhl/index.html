Discover the cooperative area as a truck driver at the Migros Aare cooperative!

Explore our beautiful region and help ensure that our products arrive in stores safely and on time.

This activity offers an ideal opportunity to have an enriching part-time job alongside your studies, family or other obligations.

  • Explore our beautiful region and help ensure that our products arrive in stores safely and on time.

  • You supply the sales points in the cantons of Aargau, Bern and Solothurn and ensure on-time deliveries

Main responsibilities

  • You drive our modern and versatile vehicle fleet (Scania, MAN, Renault Trucks) with a trailer
  • You supply the sales points in the cantons of Aargau, Bern and Solothurn and ensure on-time deliveries
  • By securing and checking the load, you ensure that it remains intact during transport
  • You carry out regular checks on the vehicle assigned to you to ensure maximum safety
  • By loading and unloading Goods and returns, you ensure an efficient interface to goods logistics

Key requirements

  • Professional experience: Ideally a few years of professional experience as a truck driver
  • With a category CE driving license, you have the necessary qualifications to drive our vehicles safely
