Role overview

This is what awaits you: Schweizer Mobiliar Asset Management AG is responsible for the asset management of the Mobiliar Group, its pension funds, funds and third-party client mandates.

In order to strengthen our customer & product management team, which is also responsible for multi-asset portfolio management, we are looking for an experienced personality as multi-asset portfolio manager who plays a decisive role in shaping active portfolio and asset allocation.

As a portfolio manager, you take on a central responsibility for the absolute and relative performance of multi-asset portfolios and contribute your market and investment expertise to decision-making processes, committees and the further development of our multi-asset approach.
