Role overview

In the Actuarial Services & Risk Modeling team, you support customers in analyzing and evaluating complex actuarial and financial risks and making them transparent using data-driven models.

The team combines actuarial expertise with quantitative modeling, regulatory understanding and modern technologies to create a well-founded basis for decision-making.

It is particularly exciting to work on a wide range of projects in which topics such as risk management, valuation, reservations, Solvency II, IFRS 17 and model validation play a central role.
