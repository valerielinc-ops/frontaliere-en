Application process

  • Conditions for submitting applications Applications must be submitted electronically via the online help desk of the City of Lugano (https://egov.lugano.ch/).
  • If you are interested in more than one competitive function, you must submit an application for each function.
  • In addition to the documents to be attached to the online application, at a later time you may be asked to submit the extract from the criminal record for private individuals (if applicable also the extract from the specific criminal record for private individuals) and the extract from the debt enforcement register.
