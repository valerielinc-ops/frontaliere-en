Main responsibilities

  • You will be responsible for the internal and general medical care of our inpatients.
  • You carry out medical examinations on symptomatic patients, determine the indication for further examinations, make diagnoses and initiate appropriate treatments.
  • You assess and treat somatic comorbidities in a psychiatric context and help to optimize somatic medication.
  • You work closely with psychiatrists, psychologists as well as the nursing and therapist team.
  • You support the psychiatrists in interpreting ECG and laboratory findings.
  • You carefully document the treatments and actively participate in interdisciplinary case discussions.

Key requirements

  • You have a specialist title in general internal medicine or a comparable qualification.
  • You are interested in working in a psychiatric environment and appreciate a holistic treatment approach.
  • You work independently, responsibly and at the same time enjoy working in an interprofessional team.
  • You are characterized by high social skills and an appreciative approach to patients.
  • You maintain an overview even in demanding situations and make well-founded medical decisions.
