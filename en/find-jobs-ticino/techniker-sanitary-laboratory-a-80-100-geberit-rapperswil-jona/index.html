Role overview

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES In your role you are responsible for sanitary laboratory services for research and development, quality management and production.

This includes in particular the following activities

  • Planning, setting up, carrying out and documenting tests on functional samples, prototype and pilot series parts
  • Carrying out competitive analyzes as well as technical assessment of the results, evaluation, interpretation and preparation of test and measurement results
  • Creation, maintenance and management of laboratory reports
  • Technical advice on development and sales
  • Ensuring and applying relevant national, international and internal standards and guidelines
  • Further development, maintenance and support of the laboratory infrastructure in one's own area of responsibility
  • Demonstration of tests for internal and external customers PROFILE You have technical vocational training and several years of professional experience.

You are also characterized by the following characteristics and skills:

  • Precise, structured and conscientious way of working
  • Enjoy learning new things and interdisciplinary teamwork
  • Knowledge of MS Office (Excel, Word)
  • Enjoy working with measuring equipment and software
  • Knowledge in the area of ​​product testing is an advantage
  • Knowledge of spoken and written English is an advantage ADDITIONAL INFORMATION For this position we only consider direct applications.
