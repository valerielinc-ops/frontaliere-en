The MUNICIPALITY OF LUGANO opens the public competition (valid for the year 2026) for the hiring of cleaners, school lunch attendants, urban cleaning workers, general workers, cashiers, drivers of light or special vehicles at the municipal administration of the City of Lugano, under the conditions of the Organic Regulations of collaborators of the City of Lugano (ROCCL) and of these competition specifications.

Application process

  • Conditions for submitting applications Applications must be submitted electronically via the online help desk of the City of Lugano (https://egov.lugano.ch/).
  • If you are interested in more than one competitive function, you must submit an application for each function.
  • In addition to the documents to be attached to the online application, at a later time you may be asked to submit the extract from the criminal record for private individuals (if applicable also the extract from the specific criminal record for private individuals) and the extract from the debt enforcement register.

Contacts

  • The Municipality will not, under any circumstances, consider applications and attachments sent via other channels (paper form, email, fax, etc.).
  • The Human Resources Division remains at your kind disposal (t. +41 58 866 70 32) of those who do not own a device (PC, tablet, smartphone, etc.) through which to apply.
  • The offers, accompanied by the following documents:. cover letter. curriculum vitae. recent photograph. study and work certificates. driving license must be sent via the online desk by midnight on Wednesday, 9 December 2026.
