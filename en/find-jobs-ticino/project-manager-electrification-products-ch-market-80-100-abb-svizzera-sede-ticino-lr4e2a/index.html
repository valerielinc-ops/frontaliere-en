Main responsibilities

  • Your role and responsibilities
  • In this role, you will be responsible for the operational execution of medium-size customer projects within the Medium Voltage Switchgear business, from order handover through delivery and close-out.
  • You will ensure disciplined project execution in line with contractual requirements, internal processes, and quality standards.
  • Acting as the central coordination point, you will closely interface international factories, engineering, production, logistics, and local stakeholders to ensure reliable delivery, cost control, and customer satisfaction.
  • The work model for the role is: hybrid #LI-hybrid
  • This role is contributing to the Medium Voltage Switchgear business within the Electrification Distribution Solutions division in Switzerland. You will be mainly accountable for:
  • Owning end-to-end project execution towards the customer, coordinating with factory-based project managers, engineering, production, and logistics to deliver according to scope, schedule, cost, and quality commitments.
  • Actively driving detailed planning, progress tracking, expediting, and interface management, with a strong focus on delivery reliability, issue resolution, and adherence to milestones.
  • Managing project financials, risks, changes, and claims in close alignment with internal stakeholders, ensuring early identification of deviations and implementation of corrective actions.
  • Leading project close-out activities, including customer acceptance, documentation completeness, and the structured capture, evaluation, and implementation of lessons learned to continuously improve project execution.

Key requirements

  • for the role:
  • You hold a degree in Electrical Engineering or a related engineering field, or a higher professional education qualification in engineering (e.g.
  • Höhere Fachschule), with strong relevance to electrical products and systems.
  • You have several years of hands-on experience managing or coordinating industrial customer projects, ideally within medium voltage switchgear, or complex engineered products.
  • You have solid practical experience applying project management methodologies (e.g.
  • IPMA, PMP, or equivalent) in an operational, delivery-focused environment.
  • You are highly proficient in project execution tools, including MS Office, MS Project, SharePoint, Teams, and SAP, and use them actively to drive transparency and control.
  • You are confident working with international factories and cross-functional teams, with strong skills in interface management, expediting, and problem solving.
  • You bring sound knowledge of contracts, change management, and claim handling, and are comfortable addressing deviations with internal and external stakeholders.
  • You communicate fluently in German and English (minimum C1 level in both), demonstrate a pragmatic, resilient mindset, and are comfortable operating in a fast-paced, execution-driven environment. More about us
  • ABB's Distribution Solutions division provides medium voltage components, switchgear, and solutions that connect and protect the evolving electrical grid, ensuring reliable and predictable energy distribution.
  • We link high-voltage systems to medium and low-voltage consumers, ensuring continuous power delivery to industrial, commercial, and residential users while enabling the integration of renewable energy sources into the grid.
