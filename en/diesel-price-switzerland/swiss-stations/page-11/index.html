How this index works

This index combines a TCS provider snapshot collected from the verified Cloud Function with the OpenStreetMap physical inventory, keeping inventory and price observations separate. It keeps every row after stable deduplication. Its contract explicitly says inventoryCompleteness: not-verified: this is not a complete census of Swiss pumps and does not imply that every retail site is present. Petrol and diesel are independent fields: when the diesel price is missing, the station remains visible with “Price unavailable for this fuel”. Collection time records when the snapshot was read; TCS does not necessarily provide the time when the pump price changed. Existing Ticino station detail links remain available; other stations are informational rows until a national URL contract exists.

Why this index helps cross-border commuters

For cross-border commuters, a useful comparison depends on the actual route, fuel type and observation date. A national price may be far from your crossing and does not include detour costs. Compare the same fuel in one currency with a dated exchange rate, account for extra kilometres, tolls and time, and check the displayed pump price before refuelling. Ticino pages still serve the border comparator: its projection remains limited to stations within 25 km of the monitored Italian municipalities, while this index preserves the combined TCS + OpenStreetMap national view.
