Role overview

As a non-profit organization, Spitex Zimmerberg AG is responsible for the care, advice, support and support of people of different age groups who are dependent on external help due to health restrictions.

We enable the inhabitants of Adliswil, Thalwil, Kilchberg, Rüschlikon and Langnau am Albis to live a self-determined life in their usual environment.

Spitex Zimmerberg AG has around 160 employees, spread over four locations and is one of the largest Spitex organizations in the Canton of Zurich.

In order to support our teams, we are mainly looking for our evening service with assignments in the day service as required by immediately: Care professional HF/FH 50-60 % Your tasks

  • Independent, self-responsible care and support of customers

  • Carry out and document intervention according to care planning
  • Consultation services
  • Ongoing review of quality and effectiveness of care
