Role overview

Tasks

  • Ensure patient-oriented and professional care in all three shifts
  • Promote and support caregivers and the team in specific, complex care situations
  • Identify practice-relevant questions and development topics of the department and set professional development goals
  • Continuously develop and secure the quality of care
  • Co-responsible for the development and implementation of concepts, guidelines, and professional changes
  • Organize and conduct case discussions
  • Actively participate in hospital-internal thematic working groups
  • Actively engage in practice development projects and ensure connection to relevant committees
  • If interested and suitable, represent the head of IPS nursing in her absence together with vocational training; take on personnel and professional leadership tasks during this time

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in nursing from Switzerland or recognized by SRK
  • Postgraduate study in IPS or foreign IPS training recognized by ZINA
  • Proficiency in leading the nursing process, conducting nursing assessments, and in Clinical Assessment
  • Knowledge of practice development methods (e.g., moderation of case discussions, coaching and providing feedback, planning and accompanying changes)
  • Knowledge in Evidence Based Nursing
  • Good IT and media application skills
  • Confident expression and good understanding of German in spoken and written form
