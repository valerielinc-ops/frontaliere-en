Main responsibilities

  • Quality control: You carry out chemical-analytical tests on food, packaging and everyday items and thus ensure compliance with quality and safety requirements
  • Non-target analysis: You analyze samples using GC-QTOF-MS/FID and UPLC-QTOF-MS with screening methods
  • Target analysis: You carry out analyzes on various measuring systems from Agilent, Thermo and AB Sciex in different matrices
  • Equipment responsibility: You maintain, maintain, operate and check analytical equipment in accordance with ISO 17025 and thus ensure smooth, standard-compliant laboratory operations
  • Laboratory management: You work with our laboratory information management system (LIMS) and ensure structured recording, evaluation and documentation of analysis data Requirements
  • Several years of experience: in analytics (LC, GC, MS)
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): as a laboratory assistant in EFZ chemistry or a bachelor's degree in chemistry
  • Specialist knowledge: You have experience in mass spectrometry and can use this knowledge in a targeted manner in everyday laboratory life
  • Digital competence: You work confidently with MS Office 365, especially with Teams and Excel, and also use macros in a targeted manner in your everyday work
  • Way of working: You approach tasks in an agile, structured and solution-oriented manner and find practicable ways to achieve your goal even in a dynamic environment
