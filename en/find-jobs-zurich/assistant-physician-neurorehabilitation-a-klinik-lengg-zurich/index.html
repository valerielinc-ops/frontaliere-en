Role overview

Tasks

  • Medical-organizational care of a broad spectrum of neurological patients in the inpatient setting under the supervision of a cadre physician
  • Responsibility for all medical matters (examination, medical therapies, diagnostics, visits)
  • Collaboration with the interdisciplinary rehabilitation team and communication with external bodies involved
  • If interested, possibility of rotation into the Department of Epileptology Requirements
  • First clinical professional experience with interest in neurorehabilitation
  • CH or EU state examinations (with FOPH recognition)
  • Very good knowledge of German (minor level C)
  • Outstanding social and professional skills with willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy.

Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life.

Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
