Description

You will become part of a growing actuarial consulting team that supports leading Swiss life and reinsurance companies in strategic transformation projects.   • Advise leading Swiss life and reinsurance companies on strategic transformation projects: system migrations, process modernization and redesign of actuarial functions.
  • Bring active expertise to multidisciplinary teams and work across all departments: actuarial, IT, finance, underwriting. Who brings the numbers, connects.
