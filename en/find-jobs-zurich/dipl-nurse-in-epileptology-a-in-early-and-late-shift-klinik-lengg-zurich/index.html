The Lengg AG clinic in Zurich is the competence center for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

Our goal is to provide patients with neurological diseases with the greatest possible rehabilitation and to ensure accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy.

Around 300 employees are humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably committed to ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life.
