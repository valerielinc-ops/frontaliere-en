Main responsibilities

  • You monitor the lodge during continuous operation, operate security-related technology and process reports and alarms.
  • You carry out regular inspection tours and ensure safety and order on the area.
  • You intervene in medical emergencies as well as fire, gas and technical alarms (HKLS).
  • You look after the telephone switchboard, forward calls and provide information.
  • You receive and register visitors and assist with general inquiries.
  • You manage the delivery and return of keys for authorized persons.
