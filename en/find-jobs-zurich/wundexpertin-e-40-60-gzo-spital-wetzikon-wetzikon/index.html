Role overview

Tasks

As a wound expert, you are responsible for caring for our clients in the wound outpatient clinic. You ensure the assessment and treatment of complex wound situations in collaboration with the medical service. You take on the consulting and training of medical staff and are the contact person for relatives, nursing homes, and home care services.

Requirements

  • Qualified nurse HF and training as a wound expert
  • Certified wound expert or similar professional training
  • Work experience in a wound outpatient clinic
  • Good IT and media application skills
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written
  • Ability to work independently and commitment to team-oriented and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Flexible and economical thinking and acting are required
