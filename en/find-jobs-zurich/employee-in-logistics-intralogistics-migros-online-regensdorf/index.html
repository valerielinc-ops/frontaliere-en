Main responsibilities

  • Intralogistics & replenishment control: Ensuring product replenishment in the various zones as well as distribution and storage of containers at defined transfer and storage locations.
  • System-supported work: Booking of warehouse movements and containers using the warehouse management system to ensure correct stocks.
  • Inventory & quality management: Detecting faulty, damaged or incorrectly stored items, correcting inventories using an MDE device and reporting and clarifying deviations.
  • Material & disposal management: Provision of materials and disposal of waste and empty containers according to orders from the system.
