Role overview

Tasks

  • You work in rotation at our various modalities and take on examinations in a versatile radiological environment.
  • Our modern equipment includes:
  • 2 DR X-ray stations (Philips)
  • Multislice-Spectral CT (Philips)
  • 2 MRI (1.5T and 3T, Philips)
  • Angiography system for diagnostic and interventional procedures (Siemens)
  • Mobile DR device for IPS and delivery room (Siemens)
  • Mammography including tomosynthesis and biopsy workstation (Hologic)
  • Doppler-capable ultrasound device

Requirements

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Radiologic Technician HF or equivalent training as MTR/MTRA with recognition or recognition capability in Switzerland
  • Enjoy working in various modalities such as CT, MRI, mammography, or conventional radiology
  • Willingness to work shifts (morning shift 7:45 AM–4:40 PM, evening shift 11:15 AM–8:10 PM, and night shift 8:00 PM–7:45 AM), without on-call duties
  • Team spirit, flexibility, and an independent working style
  • Very good German language skills, both spoken and written

We Offer

  • A cohesive team
  • We maintain a collegial and supportive collaboration.
  • New employees are carefully integrated and can count on an experienced team.
  • Modern radiology with a wide range
  • You work with contemporary infrastructure and modern modalities in a varied environment.
  • Attractive night shift model
  • Our night shift model creates longer recovery phases: after four night shifts, there are seven days off, after three more night shifts, another three days off.
  • This way, you benefit from additional blocks of free time totaling around four weeks throughout the year.

  • We support internal and external training and promote the professional development of our employees.
