Role overview

For our IT department we are looking for an System Engineer -/in (2nd level support) As a system engineer, together with our ICT team, you are responsible for the maintenance, development and renewal of our diverse systems such as virtualization (VMware) networks, Apple ecosystem (JAMF), security awareness, Azure and Office 365 and other applications.

In this role, you are also responsible for the analysis and resolution of IT disruptions in 2nd level support and contribute to the stable operation of our IT infrastructure.

In project teams, you plan, develop and integrate new services and technologies.

You also support the 1st level support for demanding technical requests.

Good technical understanding, an independent working method and the motivation to familiarize ourselves with new topics are important to us.

We offer you a modern working environment, varied tasks and the opportunity to actively contribute and develop your experience.

Every single person is at the center with their individual quality of life.

We do everything we can to ensure that our approximately 250 residents, 246 daycareers and 52 employees at protected workplaces in the Wagerenhof can enjoy the highest possible quality of life.

For employees of different backgrounds and professionalism, Wagerenhof is an attractive employer and a competent training partner for learners of all levels.

For this we give you the responsibility

  • Together with our ICT Operations team, you will supervise and maintain our IT systems
  • You are jointly responsible for the constant guarantee of security.
