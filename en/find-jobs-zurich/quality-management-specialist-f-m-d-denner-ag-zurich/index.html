Main responsibilities

  • Implementation and support of the fruit & vegetables, beverages and tobacco product groups with regard to quality requirements and legal requirements.
  • Quality-related product data such as: Ensure specifications and labels are correct.
  • Manage the tender process from a quality management perspective including annual planning of tastings and take part in offer, product range and complaint tastings.
  • Process complaints, complaints and inquiries from internal and external bodies, prepare consumer tests.
  • Carry out product maintenance and product range reviews.
  • Head of quality management for relevant topics such as projects, audits, etc. support.
