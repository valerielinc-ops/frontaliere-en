Role overview

Marché Restaurants Schweiz AG is one of the 10 most sales-strong Swiss companies in the field of full-service system catering and is the clear number one in the freeway catering with the brand "Marché".

Marché Restaurants Schweiz AG has been a separate subsidiary of the Coop Group since 2014.

With our brands and concepts – Marché, Zopf & Zöpfli, Burger King, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs – we are not only present at motorways or at Zurich Airport, but also at the largest Swiss railway stations in Zurich, Bern and Basel, as well as in some popular shopping centres in German-speaking Switzerland.
