Company and context

  • We have converted our organizational form to semi-autonomous teams, that is, we are guided by the famous Buurtzorg model, but have adapted it to our needs.
  • In an interdisciplinary team, with qualified nurses, housekeeping and support staff and case management at your side, it should be a new form of work that is up-to-date and allows for the necessary flexibility, but also structure and continuity. You have the following qualifications:
  • Completed health EFZ training or equivalent training Ideally Spitex experience Strong professional and social skills
  • self-employed and responsible working practices valid category B ticket
  • Your tasks: As a competent health care professional, you take on responsibility in the care and care including preventive support from our clients at home.
  • You independently carry out basic and treatment care according to the care plan.

  • a varied, responsible and independent task in an innovative organisation; flexible conditions of employment;
  • 5 weeks of holidays (from 50 years of age 6 weeks);
  • very good social benefits (e.g. pension fund contribution 60:40); A motivating and appreciative working environment.
