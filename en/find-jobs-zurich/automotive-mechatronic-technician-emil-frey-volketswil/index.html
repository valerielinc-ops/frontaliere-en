Role overview

The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family-owned company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive sector.

Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the pillars of the company.

The activities focus on the importation of automotive brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare parts and accessories, as well as the provision of financial services.
