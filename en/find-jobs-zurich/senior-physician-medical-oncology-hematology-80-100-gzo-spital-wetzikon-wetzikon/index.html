Role overview

Tasks

You can expect a varied interdisciplinary collaboration with internal and external physicians from other specialties, particularly within the framework of the interdisciplinary tumor board and during joint visits with representatives of the Palliative Care Team. Your main responsibilities include

  • The independent care of patients with conditions from the entire spectrum of Medical Oncology in outpatient consultations and on the inpatient wards.
  • Active participation in the training and further development of assistant physicians and nursing staff.
  • Commitment to continuous optimization in collaboration and processes.
  • Supervision of clinical studies is desired, as well as collaboration with external centers.

Requirements

  • You have completed specialist training in Medical Oncology (or equivalent with MEBEKO recognition) or are close to completion, along with a solid internal medicine background.
  • You possess current specialist knowledge, combined with high social competence and pronounced communication skills. In particular, the ability to convey a serious diagnosis with empathy and sensitivity and to support relatives.
  • You bring a high quality and service awareness, focusing on safe, efficient, and resource-saving processes.
  • You have analytical and diagnostic skills and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • You are a respectful, solution-oriented, and integrative personality who enjoys working in a team and values professional exchange.
  • You have a passion for engaging in the training and further education of the oncology workforce (physicians and nursing).
  • Excellent interpersonal skills, very good German language skills, solid IT user knowledge (Phönix, KISIM, Microsoft Suite), and a careful, structured, and reliable working style complete your profile.

We Offer

  • We offer a very interesting and diverse field of activity in a progressive, dynamic, and multiprofessional working environment characterized by high quality, respect, and appreciation.
  • With us, you can further develop and benefit from internal and external training opportunities.
  • Various benefits and internal offers are also part of our perks, such as an excellent staff restaurant, an attached daycare center, and various events for our employees.
