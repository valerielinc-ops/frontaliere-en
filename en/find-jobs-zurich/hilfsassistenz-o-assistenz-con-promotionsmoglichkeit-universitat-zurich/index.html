Main responsibilities

  • Your work at the chair depends on the state of your training and your profile.

  • Depending on the qualification and employment level, the implementation of courses as well as the supervision of students can also be added.
  • Also possible is participation in the preparation and correction of exams and in the supervision of bachelor, master and seminar projects.
  • Another focus can be, also depending on your profile, the cooperation in ongoing and the co-development of future projects in the field of legal technology and the Digital Humanities.
