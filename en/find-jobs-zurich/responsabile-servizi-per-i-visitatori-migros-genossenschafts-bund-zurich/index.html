Main responsibilities

  • Establishment and further development of an audience-oriented service and reception culture as well as conception of processes and forms of communication that enable low-threshold access
  • Systematic evaluation of visitor feedback and key figures;
  • Derivation and implementation of concrete measures such as guidelines and standards
  • Ensuring smooth operations in the Visitor Services area with responsibility for the museum shop, supervision, personnel deployment planning, representative coordination and cooperation with security, technology and facility management
  • Professional and personnel management of the Visitor Services team (motivating and supporting employees in change processes and specifically recognizing and promoting individual strengths)
  • Close cooperation in the team, especially with the areas of mediation, communication and the exhibition team

Key requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in an environment with intensive Contact with the public and leadership experience, ideally in the cultural or service sector
  • Completed studies (university/ETH, FH, HF): in a relevant area or equivalent professional experience
  • Enjoy designing and further developing processes as well as working closely in teams and transversal working groups
  • Strongly audience-oriented attitude: enthusiasm for contact with heterogeneous groups of visitors and breaking down barriers and, ideally, experience in working with target groups remote from the museum, for example in community projects
  • Interest and openness for contemporary art, topics related to cultural participation and different approaches to art and culture
  • Fair wages: Function-oriented and market-oriented remuneration - "We pay fair" certified
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Club school and leisure facilities: 70% discount on offers from the Migros Club School, 30% discount for fitness/wellness/aqua parks - leisure and cultural offerings: As the main sponsor of runs and events, Migros offers employees limited places at half price.
  • Creative and decision-making scope: Opportunity to independently shape and further develop the field of responsibility
