Role overview

For our lively business with a flower shop/garden shop/farm shop and glass house café, we are looking for you immediately or by appointment as a creative, motivated florist EFZ, BP or HFP with professional experience (100%) Do you enjoy training apprentices, are you customer-oriented and do you like to take on responsibility in day-to-day organization and purchasing?

Do you also enjoy working in an inclusive team with employees who need support?

Every single person's individual quality of life is the focus.

We do everything we can to ensure that our approximately 250 residents, 246 daycare workers and 52 employees can enjoy the highest possible quality of life in protected workplaces at Wagerenhof.

  • Development of floristic workpieces
  • Sale of gardening and floristry products
  • Technical co-responsibility of the flower shop and sales
  • Serving and advising customers
  • Purchasing cut flowers, plants and hardware at the flower exchange
  • Support in the training and supervision of learners
  • Collaboration and guidance of employees in need of support
  • Training as a florist EFZ, BP or HFP
  • Experience in purchasing and training learners
  • Working independently and responsibly
  • Solution orientation, resilience and reliability
  • Business thinking
  • Enjoy working with people
  • Varied everyday life
