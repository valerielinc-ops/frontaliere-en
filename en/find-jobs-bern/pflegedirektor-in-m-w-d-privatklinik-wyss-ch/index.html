Role overview

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy.

Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders.

The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the region of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations.

Company and context

  • The most important thing in short working as a member of our clinic management You work closely with all areas of our psychiatric clinic and shape the strategic and entrepreneurial orientation of the nursing organization.
  • You take overall responsibility for all care teams and ensure that modern, safe and patient-centered care is lived.
  • In addition to direct management of the care areas in our patient houses You also have central units such as Quality Management, Central Education of Care, Care Development, Pharmacy and Patient Disposition & Therapy Booking Office.
  • In this broad field of activity, you control both strategic and operational projects, develop nursing standards with your teams and ensure that quality, education and care are optimally integrated.
  • You lead the nursing profession through upcoming processes of change and growth in the clinic and ensure that our family, appreciative and interprofessional culture is maintained and can develop further.
