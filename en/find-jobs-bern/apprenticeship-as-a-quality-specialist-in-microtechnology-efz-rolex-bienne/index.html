Original description

Introduction Motivated by a learning of quality in microtechnology? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us! The quality engineer in microtechnology supports the production sectors to ensure their quality level. He/she controls the means and methods of dimensional and aesthetic control. Thanks to the tools and statistics applied, he/she will be able to carry out measurements, solve problems on a daily basis and manage projects to improve its scope, whether at the level of a product or a process. This learning is suitable for young people who love precise and methodical work, both alone and in a team. Profile • Skills for mathematics and technical drawing • Logical, analytical and synthetic mind • Writing skills • Good communication skills and ease in human contacts Conclusion If you are motivated to meet the challenge, do not hesitate to apply. Your file must contain a curriculum vitae, a cover letter and your school bulletins for the last three years. Boxed by experienced trainers, you can evolve in an extraordinary working environment. This training will also provide you with excellent prospects. Rolex is committed to offering you more than just learning: a real opportunity for personal and professional development.
