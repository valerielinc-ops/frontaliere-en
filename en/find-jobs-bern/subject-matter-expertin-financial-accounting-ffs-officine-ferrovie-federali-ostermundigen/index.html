Company and context

  • In the role of Control Owner, you carry out controls of the internal control system in the closing process within the Shared Service Organization Finance and thus strengthen the reliability of the financial statements.
  • Participation in cross-divisional projects brings you pleasure and you bring your expertise for efficient and compliant processes.
  • During the annual audit, you prepare the documents and answer questions from the inspection body conscientiously.
  • You create ad-hoc analyses and generate key figures so that management and departments can make informed decisions.
  • You bring that with you.
  • With clear and appreciative communication, you build trust and take responsibility for your tasks.
  • Thanks to your many years of experience (minimum 4 years) in financial accounting, you have in-depth knowledge of Swiss GAAP FER and bond law.
  • SAP knowledge is a prerequisite (S/4Hana is an advantage) you still have very good user knowledge in M365, especially Excel.
  • You have successfully completed an education at a tertiary level (FH or university) with a focus on accounting or accounting.
  • In German you are confident in negotiations (level C1 or more) and you have a good knowledge of another national language.
