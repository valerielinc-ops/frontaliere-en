Company and context

  • Entrance: &#xa0 immediately or by agreement Location: BIEL – BIENNE The Réseau de l’Arc is a health organization firmly established in the region with around 1,250 employees, providing comprehensive care for around 80,000 people.
  • It comprises two hospitals in Moutier and Saint-Imier, five Medicentres and the Medical Center Biel (MZB), supplemented by an ambulance network, two nursing homes, two radiology institutes, a center for physical and cognitive therapies and a service for hospital care at home.
  • As the first fully integrated health organization in Switzerland, the Réseau de l’Arc offers its own health insurance plan within the framework of the VIVA project, which guarantees the residents of the legal arc optimal and coordinated care.
  • The Medical Center Biel (MZB) is at the center of this integrated model and unites an interdisciplinary team of family doctors, specialists, nurses, psychologists and therapists.
  • It offers a wide range of services – from general medicine and emergency care through specialist consultations to radiology and laboratory.
  • The Medical Center Biel (MZB) and the family practice VIVA together form an integrated care system that guarantees patients comprehensive and continuous care.
  • While the MZB, as a regional centre of excellence, offers a wide range of medical services – from primary care to specialized examinations and emergency treatments – the practice VIVA complements this offer by providing long-term, personal support for the insured persons of the VIVA health plan.
  • The close integration of MZB and practice VIVA enables coordinated and patient-centred care: Acute complaints can be treated easily and quickly via the walk-in clinic of the MZB, while chronic and preventive health aspects are comprehensively monitored within the scope of family care in VIVA practice.
