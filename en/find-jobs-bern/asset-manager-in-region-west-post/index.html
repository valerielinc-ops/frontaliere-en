Role overview

As part of Post Immobilien's portfolio management, Asset Management is responsible for the strategic support and development of around 600 Post-owned properties.

We ensure that existing potential for increasing value and development is identified and developed and that the interests of the Swiss Post as a landowner with ownership and market understanding are taken care of.

For the Western Switzerland region we are looking for an experienced asset manager with a clear eye for the forward-looking and sustainable development of real estate.

  • As part of Post Immobilien's portfolio management, Asset Management is responsible for the strategic support and development of around 600 Post-owned properties.
  • We ensure that existing potential for increasing value and development is identified and developed and that the interests of the Swiss Post as a landowner with ownership and market understanding are taken care of.

Additional details

  • With you, we will further develop the Swiss Post's real estate portfolio in a future-oriented manner and translate owner goals into concrete portfolio and property decisions. This is what you can do
  • You analyze the performance of your properties, create profitability calculations and estimates, derive optimization potential and ensure the implementation of the defined return and development goals in your region. This is what you bring with you
