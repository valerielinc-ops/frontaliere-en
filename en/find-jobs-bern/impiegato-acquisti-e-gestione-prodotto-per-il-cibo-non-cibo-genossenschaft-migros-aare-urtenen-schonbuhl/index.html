Role overview

Do you have organizational talent, enjoy working in a structured manner and feel at home in food/non-food? In this versatile role, you support purchasing and product management in day-to-day business, coordinate information flows, create analyzes and actively participate in projects Mansioni

  • You independently process branch and customer inquiries and ensure that concerns are resolved efficiently, solution-oriented and on time
  • You carefully prepare documents, evaluations and the basis for decision-making and thus support day-to-day business
  • You control the flow of goods in new and converted supermarkets and VOI branches
  • You plan, process and control the quantity requirements for seasonal theme operations in the responsible product range
  • You take over the planning of regional activities and coordinate the appointments and the flow of information between branches and suppliers
  • You are jointly responsible for the maintenance of existing and introduction of new regional items and ensure compliance with the quality and packaging declaration specifications
  • You ensure the flow of information between the Food / Nonfood team, Migros Supermarket AG, regional suppliers and branches securely and are responsible for storing information as well as keeping it up-to-date on internal share points
  • Requirements
  • Ideally at least 3 years of professional experience: in administration or in retail
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): in retail or as a merchant
  • You are structured and organized, keep track of multiple tasks and communicate clearly and in an appropriate manner
  • You enjoy working in a team, are actively involved and value collaboration with different stakeholders
  • You act in a customer-oriented manner, quickly identify needs and find suitable, practical solutions
  • You know the special features of the retail trade and understand the requirements of a dynamic environment
  • You work confidently with common MS Office applications and use them efficiently in everyday work
