Role overview

The VIVA project – an innovation in Switzerland As part of the VIVA project, the Arc Network is the first fully integrated health organization in Switzerland to offer its own insurance and care plan.

VIVA is based on a modern vision of community-based medicine: a coordinated, continuous and patient-centred management that promotes collaboration among all actors in the health system.

Accessible to the inhabitants of Jura and the Jura Arc, this model guarantees an optimal quality of care and a smooth and coherent health path.

The Arc Network, which is firmly established in its region, is a major player in community care in the Jura Arc and its neighbouring regions.

It ensures a comprehensive, coordinated and interdisciplinary management of the population at the heart of a rich and diverse territory.

The network consists of two hospitals in Moutier and Saint-Imier and nine Medicentres.

The provision of care is complemented by a vast regional ecosystem including relief services, two medical-social institutions, two radiology institutes, a physical and cognitive therapy centre and a hospital-at-home service.

With nearly 1,300 employees, the Arc Network covers the outpatient and hospital needs of around 80,000 inhabitants, valuing regional wealth, human proximity and the quality of care.
