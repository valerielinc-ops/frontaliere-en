  • Introduction For our Quality & Sustainability department, we are looking for a Watchmaker Analyst whose main mission will be to carry out technical and aesthetic characterizations and audits on “serial” products and new products within the Product Audits laboratory.
  • Prepare, organize and carry out audits to test the conformity of Rolex components, sub-assemblies and movements throughout the product life cycle
