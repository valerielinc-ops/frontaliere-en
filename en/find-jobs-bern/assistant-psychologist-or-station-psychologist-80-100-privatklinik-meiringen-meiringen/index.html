Role overview

The Meiringen Private Clinic is located in the impressive landscape of the Haslital, in the Bernese Oberland.

Here people with all forms of psychological suffering find reception, treatment and care.

The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese upper country.
