Role overview

Tasks: The prerequisite is an above-average master's degree in Germanistic linguistics or other linguistics, provided that there is a capacity and interest to acquire expertise in Germanistic linguistics.

A researcher is sought, which optimally complements the team of system linguistics in research and teaching.

Accordingly, the candidate should ideally have good knowledge in several areas of system linguistics (especially phonology, morphology, syntax and semantics).

The willingness and ability to carry out independent and innovative research in the areas mentioned above is expected.

In addition, motivation, perseverance, communication and team skills as well as high reliability and good time management are required.
