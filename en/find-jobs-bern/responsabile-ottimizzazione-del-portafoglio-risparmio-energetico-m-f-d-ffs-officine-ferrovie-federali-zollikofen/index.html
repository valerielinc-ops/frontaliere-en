  • What you bring.
  • By your structured work and motivational leadership style, you organize cross-cutting collaboration, prioritize topics and structure processes in a results-oriented manner.

  • You are distinguished by excellent communication skills, a strong negotiator and you see yourself as a facilitator who creates the right framework conditions for development.

  • Your experience covers problem analysis, solution development and implementation in business processes.
